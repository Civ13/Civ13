# Hay

Click open ground next to you with grass cuttings in hand to spread up to twenty portions there to dry. Clicking the drying grass with more cuttings adds them (up to twenty) and restarts the drying. The grass dries after six dry 30-second checks, three minutes in all, and becomes the same number of hay portions. Checks under open sky while the area is wet (rain or snow) do not count; a roof or an indoor area keeps it drying. Picking the drying grass up by hand returns the cuttings and loses the progress. A save keeps the progress; a loaded batch resumes its checks.

Hay is used in three ways:

- Feed: use hay on a hungry grazing animal (an animal that eats grass, such as cattle, sheep or goats) to feed it one portion. Grazing animals also eat hay lying next to them and from a hay bale next to them, and walk to a hay bale within six tiles when there is no grass within six tiles.
- Hay bale: use ten hay in hand and choose the bale to tie a dense hay bale in front of you, or on your own tile when the front is blocked. A bale holds ten portions. Take one portion off by hand, or stuff hay back into it. It disappears when empty.
- Thatch roofing: use two hay in hand and choose thatch roofing. It needs the same knowledge as thatch roofing from leaves.
