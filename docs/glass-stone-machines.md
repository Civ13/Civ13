# Glassblowing bench and mason's saw

Code: `code/modules/1713/glass_stone_machines.dm` (on the workshop machine base in
`clay_lime_fire.dm`). Sprites: `icons/obj/glass_stone_machines.dmi`, generated by
`tests/sprites/create_glass_stone.py` (edit the script, not the DMI).
Tests: `python3 tests/glass_stone_machines/test_contract.py`. The offline native
boundary probe is `python3 tests/glass_stone_machines/bench_probe.py --byond-bin <pinned-bin>`.

| Station | Built from | Research node | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glassblowing bench | 6 wood | glassworking | Beside a reachable bloomery, blast furnace or kiln holding fuel, use glass on it and pick a ware. Each go takes the hand recipe's glass and time, burns 0.5 fuel from that fire and gives two pieces. |
| Mason's saw | 8 wood | stone_masonry | Use stone, marble or sandstone on it. Every 2 s it turns one stone or marble block, or three sandstone, into two bricks (by hand: one). It needs the brickmaking knowledge the hand recipe needs. |

- `glass_bench_wares` and `masons_saw_cuts` are shared tables; the test keeps
  the wares equal to `material_recipes_global.txt` and the saw ahead of the
  hand yield.
- Reinforced glass is refused; only plain glass sheets are blown.
- Both stations pay after the wait, check research again after it, stop for
  saves and hold no lists. Glassblowing rechecks touch reach to the bench
  and furnace and discards a choice or batch interrupted by a save or load.

Not yet verified in a native client: the sprites in game.
