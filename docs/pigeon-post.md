# Pigeon post

No wires or electricity are needed. Build a **pigeon loft** from softwood
under **Paper & Printing** where it can reach open, dry ground. Look for wild
pigeons on temperate, semiarid or savanna land.

1. Pull a live pigeon next to the loft. Right-click the loft and choose
   **Train Homing Pigeon**. Keep the pigeon with you until training finishes.
2. Lead the pigeon to a different open tile on the same level, within
   160 tiles. With **Help** intent, use a held sheet of paper on the bird.
   You can also use a closed envelope containing exactly one sheet.
3. Stay put with the bird and letter for the short release action. The
   bird and the same letter arrive together at the foot of the home loft.
   Anyone there can pick it up; use a sealed envelope for privacy.
4. The pigeon only flies home. Bring it back to the sender by hand for
   another delivery, or exchange birds trained at each settlement so
   both can write back. If a loft is destroyed or replaced, retrain its birds.

Pigeons cannot route between regions or levels. A roof, a blocked landing
tile, or open water makes the route unavailable. The loft does not store
letters or notify people elsewhere. A pigeon can be injured like other
animals, so leave it somewhere safe when not using it.

## Other ways to communicate

Post public news or job listings on a noticeboard, leave physical mail in
a mailbox, and use a bell for a local signal. By 1903, electricity also
supports wired telegraphs; see the [clinic dispatch pilot](civic-1903-clinic-dispatch.md)
for a worked example of combining public notices with a named station.
