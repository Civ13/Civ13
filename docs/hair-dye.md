# Dyeing your hair

Hold a sheet of cloth or wool cloth in your active hand. On **Help** intent,
aim at your **head** for scalp hair or your **mouth** for facial hair, then
click your own character. Pick a colour and keep holding the sheet until the
application finishes. Each finished application uses one sheet; cancelling or
choosing the existing colour costs nothing.

You use the same named colours you know from tailoring: Weaving unlocks the
first set, Classical Dress and Fine Garments add more, and Textile Mills lets
you mix a custom colour. Head and facial hair can have different colours.
Dye does not change the hairstyle or length. Use a razor to change the cut.
