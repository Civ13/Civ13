# Noticeboards

Craft the existing wood notice board. It is public without a closed cover:
anyone beside it can pin a paper, photograph, or map, read one, or remove the
original. A board takes up to five posts. Take a post down to edit it. Removing
one drops it onto the board's tile rather than making a copy.

The existing recipe places the board on the tile in front of you, including
a wall tile. For a board on a floor tile, face a solid wall directly beside
the board and use a hammer without harm intent to hang it. A wrench takes a
board you mounted with a hammer back down.

Use one glass sheet on the board to fit a cover. You can read everything through
closed glass, but must open it to add or remove posts. An unkeyed cover opens
and closes from the board panel. To lock the cover, first take the key out of a
keyed fitted lock by using the lock in hand, then attach that lock to the open
cover. Use the matching key or keychain on the board to close and lock it, or to
unlock and open it. A hammer used with harm intent smashes only the glass: the
lock stays on the frame and the original posts stay pinned. A new sheet replaces
the pane; a board without glass remains public.

Existing mapped boards keep their placements. Post contents and the fitted
cover and lock are part of the ordinary world save. Restore rebuilds the display
from the actual contents, without picking up neighboring papers.
