The first boat, available from the Stone Age (the raft stays in Carts, Bronze Age).

| Item | Recipe | Research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dugout log | 12 wood or soft wood, 20 s, one per tile | Basic Toolmaking |
| Paddle | 2 wood or soft wood | Basic Toolmaking |

## Making it

The log is worked in three rounds. Each round: char the top with any open flame (a lit torch,
match, lighter; `isflamesource()`, 8 s), then scoop out the charred wood with a stone adze
(12 s) or any hatchet-quality tool (18 s). Scooping raw wood is refused. After the third scoop the
log becomes a canoe. Finishing counts as crafting practice for the log's node.

## Using it

The canoe is a raft subtype, so boarding, leaving, river drift, boathouses and agent boats treat
it like a raft.

- Two people (`mobcapacity` 2) and one crate (`storagecapacity` 1). One person sits amidships; two
  sit fore and aft along the hull (6 px each way), lifted 5 px while it lies east or west.
  `seat_paddlers()` runs on every turn (`updatepassdir`) and tile step, and
  `vehicle_offset_seat()` puts whoever gets on at the hull's own pixel offset.
- Step delay 14, or 8 while the steerer holds a paddle in either hand (a raft is 12).
- Refuses deep salt water (open sea), and any salt water while the wind there is 3 or more
  (`weather_wind_at`). Fresh water is always fine. A hammer takes it apart for 6 wood when it is empty and out of deep water.
- 4 wood and then 3 rope make it an [outrigger canoe](outrigger-canoe.md).
