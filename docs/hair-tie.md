# Tie or let down hair

When your scalp hair reaches your shoulders, use **IC → Tie / Let Down Hair**.
This is reversible, costs no items, and doesn't change your haircut, saved
style, beard or dye. Examine yourself to see whether your hair is tied back.
Other players can see the tied-back status on examine unless your head is
covered. There is no new tied-back sprite in this version.

On civilization maps:

- Loose long hair dries 10% more slowly after rain or swimming. Tying it back
  returns drying to its usual rate. Drying by a lit fire is unchanged.
- Loose, uncovered long hair adds 5% to the time to mine rock or work a seam
  with a pickaxe. Tying it back or wearing something that covers your head
  (including a raised hood) avoids the extra time. Ice breaking, yields,
  skills and protection from the weather are unchanged.

A new scalp haircut removes the tie; trimming your beard doesn't. The tie
carries through character saves and supported region transfers. On an older
region build the hair arrives loose instead of blocking the trip.
