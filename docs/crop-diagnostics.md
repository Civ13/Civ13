# Reading crop condition

Examine a planted crop to inspect it. Farming skill controls how much you can tell:

- Below 1.3, examine gives the normal crop description without farming diagnostics.
- At 1.3, well-nourished living crops can show a possible extra-yield hint. Dead crops
  instead explain that they cannot be harvested and should be uprooted before replanting.
- At 1.6, living crops show qualitative water condition and guidance for depleted soil.
- At 2.2, living crops show numerical water and nutrition levels.

Mushrooms do not need watering, so their diagnostics say that instead of showing 0/0.
Static display plants do not give farming advice. Soil condition describes the crop's
last sampled nutrition; adding compost is reflected when its next growth cycle samples
soil. Extra yield remains random. Diagnostics do not speed growth, water a plant, change
seasons or allow an unsupported crop to grow in a biome.

Use the existing hatchet, bayonet, knife or scythe interaction to harvest a ready crop
or uproot an unready/dead crop. Harvesting removes the plant; crops are not perennial.
