# Campfire-built stone kiln

A player with Basic Furniture can use ten stone blocks on an extinguished, empty ordinary campfire and spend 150 deciseconds building the existing stone-kiln family. The cost and construction time match the global stone-kiln recipe; the upgrade instead uses the campfire's tier-zero gate. The ordinary kiln recipe remains gated by Bronze Working. The fire must have no stove pot or contents. The kiln starts unlit with the campfire's remaining fuel and wood-ash accounting transferred; it does not receive the campfire's default four fuel a second time. This is provisional balance and is not a fast emergency desert route: hand-sifting ten stone alone takes thirty uninterrupted minutes, and research and other resources are separate dependencies.

The upgraded kiln retains the existing kiln's metallurgy behavior, but inserting or firing a loaded mold still requires Bronze Working; the lower construction gate grants no metallurgy research. It can hold one open, nonflammable small pot containing only water, salt, cholera and food poisoning. A paid kiln cycle removes the two biological reagents and leaves the water and salt. Alternatively, during an active kiln cycle a player holding a wooden mug and a stick or handle can use a stone placed on the kiln's turf to heat the mug away from the flame. The kiln cycle costs two fuel units, and this separate ten-second hot-stone action costs one additional unit; it requires that unit to remain available after the kiln starts. A filled flammable vessel used normally is kept out of the fire and gives a hot-stone hint; using it with harm intent deliberately burns the vessel and its contents. A suitable glass vessel can still transfer recognized liquid fuel normally. The pot is placed inside the kiln and later removed; wooden mugs are not heated directly. Irradiated water and other mixtures are not treated by either route. Existing stove salt harvesting and laboratory distillation remain unchanged.

The iron heat source's existing energy contract is shared with the upgraded kiln. Sixty heat-source burn units are converted to one kiln fuel unit: wood, planks, bamboo, sticks and dung give 60 units each; coal 240, charcoal 80, and leaves 30. Other items already marked flammable use the heat source's existing 25-unit fallback, per item or stack unit. The same seven liquid reagents accepted by that heat source yield 30 units per reagent unit; nonfuel liquid is left in its container. This represents existing game behavior, not an assessment of real-world fuel suitability. The combustion engine's liquid-fuel lists, nuclear reactor fuel and other specialized machinery do not define a general kiln burn conversion and are not silently treated as the same energy source. A normal kiln cycle consumes two kiln fuel units; the old campfire has its own normal fuel consumption until conversion.

The construction action checks the active stone stack, exact campfire, research, empty contents, fuel-bearing fire state, fire-toggle identity and persistence admission before and after waiting. It checks the actual stack debit instead of relying solely on the material-stack `use()` return value. Pending fire callbacks reject deleted campfires. A kiln cycle saved or loaded during its wait is not resumed, and its already spent fuel is not refunded. The source checks in `tests/stone_kiln` are offline projections and do not prove native gameplay, callback behavior, save round trips, visual interaction or fuel balance.
