# Staying warm in winter

Cover exposed body parts and wear a coat over your ordinary clothing. Overlapping
layers reduce cooling further, while shorts and loincloths protect only the
regions they cover. Ordinary clothing helps in temperate winter; its cold rating
is 240 K (about -33 C), so it stops insulating below that temperature. Check the
conditions and seek better cold-weather equipment for extreme climates.

Rain and immersion make you wet, reducing clothing insulation and accelerating
cooling. Move into dry shelter to avoid further soaking. A lit nearby brazier,
campfire, iron furnace or fireplace provides warmth; warm conditions dry you faster
and help you recover from hypothermia. Shelter alone may still be cold.

Complete coverage buys time but cannot provide permanent immunity. Clothing
below its temperature rating cannot stop cooling, and a dangerously cold body
still takes harm even when every body part is covered. Use warmth and shelter
before your condition becomes severe.

The regression measurements in [the verification guide](../tests/weather/README.md)
use accelerated nominal Life calls with a damage adapter. They do not promise
real player survival times: injuries, medicine, movement and server timing also
affect gameplay.
