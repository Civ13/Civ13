# Outrigger canoe

Code: `code/modules/naval/outrigger_canoe.dm`, with hooks in `code/modules/1713/dugout_canoe.dm`
(`canoe_work()` from the dugout's `attackby`, `dress_hull()` from its `update_icon`, `step_delay()`
from its `processmove`). Sprites: `icons/obj/vehicles/outrigger.dmi` and `icons/obj/vehicles/raft.dmi`,
generated by `tests/sprites/create_small_boats.py` (edit the script, not the DMIs; `--check` verifies,
`--preview <png>` writes a review sheet). Probe: `python3 tests/ecology_spawning/run_linux.py . tests/dugout_canoe/probe.dm`.

`/obj/structure/vehicle/raft/dugout/outrigger` is a dugout subtype, so boarding, leaving, drift,
leaks, boathouses and agent boats treat it like a dugout. The plank "outrigger boat frame" recipe is
retired; the old `/obj/structure/vehicle/boat` types still exist for maps and the RHIB.

## Building

Boat work is done with the canoe empty, ashore or in the shallows (`canoe_work_refusal()`).

1. A finished, undamaged dugout canoe takes 4 wood: `booms_fitted`, sprite `outrigger_fitted`. She will
   not move in this state. A hammer knocks the booms off again (4 wood back).
2. 3 rope lashes them: `become_outrigger()` replaces the dugout with an outrigger canoe.

A hammer takes a dugout (6 wood) or an outrigger (8 wood, 3 rope, her sail, engine and tank) apart.

## The canoe

- Two people and one crate, seats 8 px fore and aft of the middle tile.
- Step delay 13, or 7 with a paddle in the steerer's hand.
- May cross deep salt water (`open_sea`), and refuses salt water only in wind 4 (`rough_wind`).
  A raft's own bar on the Caribbean sea area still applies.
- The sheet is 96x96 with the hull on the middle tile, drawn with `pixel_w`/`pixel_z` of -32, so
  she stands on one tile and `pixel_x`/`pixel_y` stay free for the pixel movement code.

## Sail

A small sail (cloth or wool, now on Oared Ships) used on her steps a mast: `mast`, `sail_path` (the
sail given back by the Strike Mast verb or by taking her apart). The steerer's Set or Furl Sail verb
toggles `sail_set`. Set, `step_delay()` takes the lower of the paddle's pace and
`sail_step_time(src, heading)` (naval/points_of_sail.dm), so the wind only ever helps.

## Outboard

An internal combustion engine of 100 to 400cc dragged onto her (no mast) is clamped on, then a fuel
tank of up to 150 units is used on her. Fuel is poured in from any glass container. The steerer's
Start or Stop Engine verb toggles `motor_on`. Under power a tile takes `max(3, round(8 - cc / 80))`
and burns `cc / 1000` units; a dry tank stops the engine. A wrench takes engine and tank off. A canoe
carries a mast or an outboard, not both.

Not built yet: hauling weights and log rollers on land, and wear from use (lashings, soaking, torn
sails; the `mast_torn` and `log_rollers` sprites are drawn for them).
