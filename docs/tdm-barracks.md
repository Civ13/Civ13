# TDM Barracks and battle worlds

TDM runs as a region group of two worlds (shard kinds `BARRACKS` and `BATTLE`,
code in `code/modules/tdm_barracks/`):

- **Barracks** (port 1714, listed on the hub): always up and never rebooted for
  a round. Players arrive here, practise, follow the battle, and keep their record.
- **Battle** (port 1715, hidden from the hub): runs the TDM rounds and the map vote.

Val chose the ports and the name on 2026-11-08: the Barracks keeps the address
everyone already uses, so players never hit a dead port while a map loads.

## How players move

1. The battle world reports its state to the Barracks every 10 seconds, and at
   once when its phase changes: map, era, mode, phase (`pregame`, `playing`,
   `finished`, `returning`, `rotating`), countdown or round clock, players per
   side alive and fielded, the win condition text and scores.
2. When a battle forms (`pregame`), the Barracks announces it with a join link.
   **Join Battle** links the player to the battle world, where they ready up and
   pick a job on its usual pregame screen; during a round the same link joins late.
   **Auto-join Battle** sends the player to every new battle when it forms.
3. When the round ends the battle world shows its end screen and runs the map
   vote as before. At reboot it sends every player's round to the Barracks,
   links everyone back to the Barracks, and only then reboots, after waiting for
   the voted map to compile (see the shard-group README).
4. **Return to Barracks** (OOC) leaves a battle early.

Joining wakes a sleeping battle world through the doorman, and both worlds use
the routing relay when the group has one (`shard_group.send_player`).

## Records

At the end of each round the battle world sends, per player: key, character
name, side, kills, deaths, seconds alive on a side, and win, loss or draw (the
round's winner against the player's side; no winner is a draw). A kill counts
for the last human who harmed the dead (`last_harmed`, else `lastattacker`),
never for a team kill. The Barracks adds each round to the player's record in
`data/tdm_barracks/stats.json` (rounds, wins, losses, draws, kills, deaths,
best single battle, time in battle, auto-join) with the last 10 battles. This
file, the faction roster, the orders boards and the race records are all the
Barracks keeps; neither world saves.

Players see it with **My Battle Record** and **Leaderboard** (most kills, most
wins, best kill ratio over 5+ battles, best single battle, most time in battle),
and the **Battle Status** panel shows the live battle and recent battles.

## Running it

See [the shard-group README](../scripts/shard-group/README.md#tdm-barracks-and-battle-worlds)
and `scripts/shard-group/tdm.example.json`. The reports travel over loopback
with the group token `config/tdm-token.txt`, so both worlds must run on one host.

## The Barracks map

`maps/special/tdm_barracks.dmm` (three levels of 100 x 80, map ID `TDM_BARRACKS`) is
generated by `python3 tools/tdm_barracks/build_map.py`; edit the script, not the
map, and `--check` reports a stale map. The layout came from a design pass on
2026-11-08 (project file `tdm-barracks/layout-design.md`):

- North: a rifle range (firing line on the west, scored targets 10 to 60 tiles
  out, distance signs) and a four-lane pistol range.
- Middle: a grenade and mortar pit (dummies and crates come back by themselves),
  an obstacle course with a timed start and finish pad (trench, sandbags, mud,
  wire, barriers), a melee yard and gym, and a team skirmish arena with red and
  blue ready rooms and a flag.
- Building: the armory (crates and racks by era, and quartermaster kit lockers
  for 1713, 1873, 1943, 1969 and 2013), the war room (battle status board), the
  arrival hall (spawn, deployment gate, training-rooms console), the hall of
  fame (leaderboard, service record, recent battles) and a medical training bay
  with a locked admin room.
- South: a duelling ring with stands, a mess hall, a tavern and a football
  pitch (goals keep a Red-Blue score; the pitch lockers hand out boots and a
  jersey and put the player on that side for passes and tackles).

Stairs in the arrival hall lead to level 2, the annex (design pass on
2026-11-08, project file `tdm-barracks/annex-design.md`):

- The Red and Blue conference rooms, a briefing room and a coordinators'
  office. Faction doors and the rooms themselves admit only the faction on
  the roster, coordinators and admins (`barracks_annex.dm`). Admins and
  coordinators keep the roster on the board in the briefing room
  (`data/tdm_barracks/factions.json`) and write the orders boards
  (`data/tdm_barracks/orders.json`).
- A race circuit with pit garages (consoles build fuelled cars in their bays),
  a start/finish line, a drag strip, a club crossover, a gravel rally stage,
  race control with countdown races, and lap boards
  (`data/tdm_barracks/race_records.json`, `barracks_racing.dm`).
- An airfield with a 41-tile runway, four hangar consoles that build light
  planes, a fire shed, a fuel farm and a control tower with the flight board.

Level 3 is open sky over the circuit and airfield (black wall over the wing).
Planes take off from the runway into it and fly the seven air gates for a
timed sky lap; the black wall is a soft edge for aircraft. Vehicles left empty
and still on the circuit or airfield for ten minutes are cleared away.

Safe areas (`/area/caribbean/barracks`, `.../range`) make everyone in them
unhurtable; the lanes, yards, pit and arenas (`.../range/lanes`,
`.../combat`) do not. Respawn is immediate and the round never ends. The
Barracks areas are always lit (`dynamic_lighting = FALSE`), so they cost no lighting overlays.
