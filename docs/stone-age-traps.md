# Stone Age traps

Code: `code/modules/1713/stone_age_traps.dm`. Sprites: `icons/obj/stone_age_traps.dmi`,
generated by `tests/sprites/create_stone_age_traps.py` (edit the script, not the DMI).
Tests: `python3 tests/stone_age_traps/test_source.py`.

Trap lines give hunters something that works while they are away. All recipes are open
from the Stone Age to the end of the game.

| Item | Recipe | Research | Set on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Snare | 2 rope | Basic Toolmaking | Outdoor grass, dirt, sand or snow |
| Deadfall trap | 2 stone | Basic Toolmaking | Outdoor grass, dirt, sand or snow |
| Weir stakes | 6 wood or soft wood | Basic Toolmaking | Outdoor shallow fresh water (not deep, salty, frozen or flood water) |
| Pit cover | 3 wood | Field Defenses | Dig with a shovel or digging stick in the other hand |

## Snares and deadfalls

Use the kit on the ground next to you (4 s) to set it. A set trap does not wait for a real
animal. Each time someone looks at it or uses it, it rolls once for every 10 minutes since the
last roll (at most 72 rolls, 12 hours). The chance per roll is 10% plus 4% for each living animal
within 7 tiles, at most 35%, halved in winter. The first success springs the trap with:

- Snare: rabbit 75%, fox 25%.
- Deadfall: rabbit 50%, fox 35%, wild boar 15%.

Use the trap with an empty hand to take the catch (a dead animal to butcher; counts as foraging
field work). A catch left more than a day is gone to scavengers. A sprung trap is reset with an
empty hand (2 s); a set trap is taken back up the same way (3 s).

Times are real minutes since 2000 stored as text, so traps keep working across saves and cost
nothing while nobody is near them.

## Fish weir

Set like a snare but in water. Every 15 minutes it has a 35% chance (half in winter) to gain a
fish, up to 4. No stocking or feeding, unlike the fish pen. Each fish comes out of the fishing
ground the weir stands in ([fishing grounds](fishing-grounds.md)): in a fished-out stretch it stays
empty until fish return, and scooping gives the fish of that water (salmon or plain fish). Use it with an empty hand to scoop the
fish out, or, when empty, to pull it up (6 s).

## Pit traps

Hold a shovel (15 s) or digging stick (25 s) in the other hand and use the pit cover on an
adjacent outdoor ground tile: it digs the pit and hides it under branches. Punji sticks used on an
uncovered pit plant stakes. A pit cover used on an open pit covers it again; a shovel or digging
stick fills an open, empty pit in (10 s, stakes returned).

A covered pit looks like branches to anyone outside the digger's tribe; the digger and their
tribe never fall in and see it as their pit trap on examine. Anyone else who walks onto it, and any
animal of medium size or larger, falls in:

- People: knocked down, 5 brute to a leg or foot, plus 20 brute from stakes (armour applies).
  Someone on walk intent has a 50% chance to feel the cover give and catch themselves instead,
  which uncovers the pit.
- Animals: 5 brute, plus 20 from stakes.

The victim is held in the pit. A person resists to climb out (10 s); someone else can pull them
out with an empty hand (5 s). An animal scrambles out after 15 minutes if nobody kills it. The
pit is left open after a fall. Being held is not saved: a world load frees anyone in a pit.
