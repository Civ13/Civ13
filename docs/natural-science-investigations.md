# Natural Science investigations

Natural Science investigations are repeatable practice for book-research worlds. They do not require an active chapter, a research desk, Swimming, or the once-per-character **Record a Find** activity. Keep using a specimen box for practice below level 24; that method still stops at 24.

- **24 to 36: compare samples.** Hold one sample in each hand and select **IC > Investigate Samples**. Compare two different raw ores (for example iron ore and copper ore), two open vessels containing different kinds of water (for example fresh and salty water), or an unfired clay item and its matching kiln-fired form. The investigation uses one unit of each ore or liquid, or one unfired clay piece. The fired control is kept. Water evidence is based on its current in-game contents, not a container's label; collect from banks or shore without swimming.
- **36 to 60: investigate processing.** Use the same verb to compare a raw material and its matching result, such as iron ore and a bloom, copper ore and a copper ingot, or sand and an ordinary glass sheet. It uses one unit of each. These use existing products from kilns, smelters and ovens; samples may be obtained from another player. If a laboratory reaction bench is available, load and secure a beaker and select **Investigate Reaction** from the bench's verbs. It uses the normal in-game recipe, catalyst/heat and output-space rules. No credit is given for an unsuccessful reaction. The laboratory route is optional; you can reach 60 using processed samples alone.
- **Credit comes from completing the study.** Each investigation takes 12 seconds of uninterrupted work, then 30 seconds before you can start another. The first study of a recipe or exact comparison earns up to 1.8 Natural Science. The same evidence earns up to 0.6 even if alternated with other comparisons. Reversing your hands does not make a comparison new. Around 21 distinct investigations (7 basic, 14 advanced) can cover both bands; repeating accessible experiments is slower. No Mathematics, fieldwork, chapter credit or progression past 60 comes from this feature.

The world's normal character save preserves a bounded list of investigated evidence. Updated regions carry it during character travel. Travel through a region running an older build may retain your skill but lose the new repetition history. The short interval between studies is local to the current server session, not a durable timer.
