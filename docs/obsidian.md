# Obsidian

Code: `code/modules/1713/obsidian.dm`. Sprites: `icons/obj/obsidian.dmi`, generated by
`tests/sprites/create_obsidian.py` (edit the script, not the DMI).
Tests: `python3 tests/obsidian/test_source.py`.

Obsidian is volcanic glass. It is found at volcanoes in every age, from 5000 BC on,
and knaps into tools that cut harder than flint but chip sooner and do worse on armour.

## Sources

| Source | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Outcrops | About one dry lava tile in four (fixed by position) shows black glass. Grab intent on it for 5 s pries a nodule loose; 3 per tile, refilled 30 minutes after the last one. The overlay and count are runtime state, so they add nothing to a saved world. |
| Mining | A mined wall within 15 tiles of lava or dry lava, on its own level or the level above, has a 15% chance of 4-8 rough obsidian (doubled in special caves). Independent of the ore table. This replaced the old jungle rule (20%, ages 1-3). |
| Cooling lava | Lava that hardens into dry lava leaves a nodule 10% of the time, or 30% when it touches water. |

Lava and dry lava register themselves in 16-tile cells (`obsidian_volcano_cells`), so the mining
check is a few distance checks, not a range scan. A hammerstone (a stone or an unsharpened flint)
on rough obsidian knocks off one nodule; a chisel and hammer still cut it into building blocks.

## Knapping

A hammerstone on a nodule. Each option is 5 points easier than its flint version, divided by the
crafting skill as usual; a failure leaves an obsidian shard.

| Option | Time | Fail | Result | Research |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sharp flake | 4 s | 5% | Flake, force 12, sharp | - |
| Five arrowheads | 6 s | 20% | 5 obsidian arrowheads (flint gives 3) | - |
| Knife | 8 s | 30% | Obsidian knife | - |
| Spearhead | 8 s | 25% | Spearhead; use it on a branch for a spear | - |
| Scraper | 5 s | 10% | Obsidian scraper | Basic Toolmaking |
| Blade core | 10 s | 25% | Blade core | Stone Arms |

An antler pick or a bone used on a blade core presses off 6 prismatic blades (8 s); 15% of the time
the core snaps and gives 2.

## Tools against flint

Knapped tools use the `volcanic glass` material (hardness 70, integrity 60, so tool health 6;
display name "obsidian"). Flint is hardness 50, integrity 110 (health 11). The `obsidian` building
material is unchanged.

| Tool | Flint | Obsidian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrow | `DAMAGE_MEDIUM`, armour `ARMOR_CLASS` | `DAMAGE_MEDIUM+2`, armour `ARMOR_CLASS/2` |
| Atlatl dart | `DAMAGE_MEDIUM+8`, `ARMOR_CLASS` | `DAMAGE_MEDIUM+10`, `ARMOR_CLASS/2` |
| Knife | force 5, health 11 | force 7, health 6 |
| Spear | force 22, health 11 | force 25, health 6 |
| Scraper | force 7, +1 hide | force 8, +1 hide |
| Prismatic blades | - | stack of 6, force 5, sharp |

Obsidian arrowheads fit arrows and atlatl darts, not crossbow bolts. Butchering and skinning take
25% less time with an obsidian knife, scraper, flake or blade in either hand.
