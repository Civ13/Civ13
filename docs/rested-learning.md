# Rested learning

Sleep in a proper bed for at least **10 real minutes**, then wake up
in a bed to learn skills **15% faster for 45 minutes**. Time offline
while your character is asleep counts toward the rest. Once you wake,
the bonus expires on the usual clock even if you disconnect or the
server restarts.

You still have to practice to earn progress. This bonus affects skills
such as Crafting, Farming, Medicine and Swimming; it does not grant
research points or increase Strength, Stamina, Dexterity or Magic gains.
Normal skill caps and milestones still apply. Bedrolls and chairs do not
qualify, and multiple rests refresh rather than stack the bonus.
