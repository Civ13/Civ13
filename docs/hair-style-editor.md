# Custom hairstyles

With a razor in your active hand, use a non-harm intent and target your own or
a nearby character's head, eyes or mouth. Open **Design Style** in the Haircut
window. Pick a source style for the **Crown and bangs**, **Sides**, and **Back
and nape**, then turn the preview to inspect each side. **Now** shows the
current look; **Styled** shows the proposed result.

The editor uses the installed hairstyle art, not new drawing layers. Every
stock style with a standing sprite can be used as a source if the character
meets its normal length, species and gender rules. Different areas use the
matching part of their selected sprite, so a mix may look different from
every direction. Headwear is not shown in the preview.

**Apply style** changes appearance without changing hair length, colour or
facial hair. **Restore base cut** removes the arrangement. A new scalp
haircut replaces it, but trimming the beard does not. The normal **Cut
Hair** tab still performs the timed cut and changes real hair length.

A stylist can save up to eight named patterns and preview a saved pattern on
any eligible character. Saving a pattern does not change that character's
hair. A pattern isn't applicable until all three pieces are available to
the recipient. Patterns are private to the character who saves them.

Custom styling and named patterns are part of the character save. They can
travel to updated regions; older builds show the normal cut and don't retain
the newer styling data if they later re-encode the character.
