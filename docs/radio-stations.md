# Radio stations (1903)

Powered radio program desks, receivers and passive antennas become craftable at
1903 (ordinal 5), retaining their paid materials, crafting skill and research
requirements. Television is available from 1969; telephones, terminals and long-range relays
retain their Modern-era availability. See [Television stations](television-stations.md).

Connect a laid adjacent cable by dragging it onto the desk and receivers. Place
an anchored antenna beside the desk. In Equipment, enable power and tune both
desk and receivers to the same frequency (150-300). Receivers work within 60
tiles on the same level; people hear them within three tiles.

Choose Present live at the desk, then speak normally. Only the selected
presenter airs, while conscious, online and within one tile. Stop presenting
ends the session. Leaving the desk or switching it off clears the presenter.
Power loss or loss of the antenna silences broadcasts. There is a one-second
line limit and a 32-listener cap; deaf and sleeping listeners are excluded.
Station name and optional faction tag are display-only and survive saves.
Live presenter references and cooldown clocks do not survive saves.

Live lines use normal speech validation, dictionary replacements, safe text,
language comprehension and the radio chat filter. Each emitted live/course
line is admin-logged. Powered keyed long-range relays can carry live station
speech to other regions using existing wear, garbling and transport limits;
remote powered receivers must be within 60 tiles of the receiving relay.
Regional arrivals never grant skill credit.

The existing crafting, farming and medical courses now work on radio from
1903. Prepare a course with at least 100 raw skill using Equipment > Program.
Six lessons air ten seconds apart in their scheduled minute each ten minutes:
crafting at 1:00 (Fall/Winter), farming at 3:00 (Spring/Summer), and medical at
5:00 (all seasons). Fixed/no-cycle maps allow all courses. Each unique lesson
is credited once per character, with the existing 125 skill cap.

See [recipe-node lessons](radio-node-lessons.md), [recipe manuals](recipe-knowledge.md)
and [television cameras](television-stations.md) for their additional controls.
