# Clay, lime and fire

Code: `code/modules/1713/clay_lime_fire.dm`. Sprites: `icons/obj/workshop_machines.dmi`,
generated by `tests/sprites/create_clay_lime_fire.py` (edit the script, not the DMI).
Tests: `python3 tests/clay_lime_fire/test_contract.py`.

Four stations that sit beside the existing hand routes. Nothing by hand was
removed or slowed.

| Station | Built from | Research node | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Potter's wheel | 6 wood | pottery_storage | Use clay on it and pick a vessel. Each takes a third of the hand time and the same clay; it repeats until the clay runs out or the worker leaves. |
| Brick mould bench | 6 wood | brickmaking | Use 4 clay on it for 8 unfired clay blocks or bricks in 10 s. With 2 quicklime in its tub the same 4 clay makes 8 unfired cement bricks (4 clay each by hand). |
| Bellows | 3 leather | bronze_working | Pump with an empty hand beside a bloomery, blast furnace or kiln. A firing started within 90 s takes 75% of the time and gives 0.5 fuel back. |
| Lime kiln | 20 stone | stone_masonry | Load up to 20 stone or marble and fuel, light by hand; 40 s later it leaves one quicklime per block. |

## Rules

- The wheel's wares (`potters_wheel_wares`) and the bench's batches
  (`brick_bench_batches`) are shared tables. The test keeps the wheel's clay
  and time equal to the rows in `material_recipes_global.txt`, and fails if a
  new clay vessel recipe is missing from the wheel.
- Stations check the worker's research before the list is shown and again
  after each wait, pay only after the wait, and stop for saves.
- The stations hold no lists. `busy` and a furnace's `bellows_until` are
  runtime state and are never saved (`bellows_until` is in
  `persistence_world_time_stamps`); the bench saves only its quicklime count
  and the kiln its two charge counts.
- The bellows override `/obj/structure/furnace/start_smelt()` and put
  `smelt_time` back as soon as the firing is scheduled, so a furnace never
  keeps or saves the shorter time.
- The lime kiln is a furnace subtype for fuel, lighting and the bellows, but
  it is off the factory ports and never accepts ore.
