# Water treatment: first survival slice

At base `3d35f802e580ac4f4d012a4ffc448bd485b62b53`, water in an open wooden mug may be boiled by heating a stone on a lit campfire or active campfire-built stone kiln and moving it with a held stick or handle. The mug is held away from the fire. The action takes 10 seconds, consumes one additional fuel unit on completion, and retains the stone and tool. These costs and the duration are provisional gameplay balance, not real-world instructions. The campfire's ordinary burning or the kiln's firing also consumes fuel. No water is created or evaporated by this action.

The mug must contain water and may additionally contain only salt, cholera and food poisoning. Completion removes the last two reagents; salt remains. Irradiated water and all other reagents are rejected, not silently treated. This does not desalinate water or establish that every possible hazard has been removed. The ordinary small-pot boiling, salt-harvesting and laboratory distiller paths are unchanged.

The global recipe makes a wood mug from one log and a campfire from four logs, gated respectively by Pottery & Cookware and Basic Furniture, separate tier-zero nodes. A stone stack must be placed on the fire's turf. Sifting uncovered, non-mineable dirt or sand by hand yields one stone in three uninterrupted minutes; a sieve shortens it to two. A stick can be made by hand from a branch-bearing tree: break a branch, remove leaves if present, then strip twigs. A crafted handle is an alternative, but needs a plank and Basic Toolmaking; splitting logs into planks itself needs a hatchet or saw. A stone hatchet needs two stone and a handle, so do not assume that recipe bootstraps itself. The separate flint-axe route requires a sharpened stick, sharpened flint and binding. The ordinary tree path accepts a hatchet or knife. Water must independently be collected in the mug, and the fire must have fuel. Cacti have no wood yield, and the Joshua tree definition has no branches, although the jungle-tree destruction path yields seven logs. Local trees, tools and usable water are map-dependent. This chain therefore does **not** prove that a dehydrated desert spawn can reach the route in time, or that its ingredients are locally available. Do not count animals, milk, clay or cactus water as prerequisites or assume they are present.

The action snapshots the mixture and exact mug holder, stone, tool and fire; it rechecks ownership, contents, fuel, fire state, fire toggle epoch and persistence admission after the wait. Accepted persistence operations replace a process-local identity, so a complete save during the wait also cancels. While busy, the mug's reagent-change callback replaces a separate identity: ordinary holder add, remove and delete operations, including an empty-and-identical-refill, invalidate the wait. The holder must still point to this mug. Busy fields and identities are excluded from world saves. These guards cover normal holder mutation callbacks, not arbitrary direct edits to reagent datum fields that bypass the holder API. Actual full-game interaction and save/load behavior remain to be verified separately.

Cloth, sand and charcoal filters require an explicit sediment or impurity model before they can have distinct effects and maintenance costs.
