# Building AOTD cars

Research Automobiles and Electricity. Craft an automobile workshop from 30 steel
in the production category. Keep a dry, empty square northeast of the workshop:
3x3 for Piccolino, Quattroporte and Kazoku, or 4x4 for the other models.

Feed material stacks into the workshop and drag four unused, unattached wheels
onto it. Drag a laid, adjacent power cable onto the workshop to connect it.
The cable network must supply 20 available power during the assembly operation.

| Inputs | Small cars | Large cars |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Steel | 40 | 60 |
| Glass | 6 | 8 |
| Cloth | 4 | 6 |
| Cable | 10 | 10 |
| Wheels | 4 | 4 |

Click the workshop, select a model and remain adjacent during assembly. A blocked
bay, interrupted work or lost power leaves inputs unspent. Unload inputs returns
unused materials. Stock persists through world saves; reconnect power after load.
Finished vehicles have empty tanks and need the engine's accepted fuel.
All nine existing AOTD layouts are available, including police and unmarked
variants. Manufacturing does not supply their map-spawn fuel or loose accessories.
