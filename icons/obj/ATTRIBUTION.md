## guns/parts.dmi

The `bipod` and `weapon_light` states (drawn under a rifle) were drawn for Civ13
(2026-11-08) and are under the same terms as the rest of Civ13's art.

## clothing/diving.dmi and icons/mob/diving.dmi

All states (`diving_dress`, `diving_helmet`, `hard_dive_suit`, `hard_dive_helmet`,
`diving_bottles`; item icons and the four-direction worn sprites) were drawn for
Civ13 (2026-11-12), pixel by pixel from a fixed palette by
`tests/sprites/create_diving_gear.py`. No borrowed or generated artwork. They are
under the same terms as the rest of Civ13's art.

## icons/mob/sea_life.dmi

All states (`kelp_turtle`, `kelp_turtle_dead`, `reef_eel`, `reef_eel_hidden`,
`reef_eel_dead`, `drift_jelly`, `drift_jelly_dead`, `shoal_dead`) were drawn for
Civ13 (2026-11-12), pixel by pixel from fixed palettes by
`tests/sprites/create_sea_life.py`. No borrowed or generated artwork. They are
under the same terms as the rest of Civ13's art. The living shoals use the
existing `fish1` and `fish2` states of `icons/mob/fish.dmi`.

## firefighting.dmi

All states (`fire_beater`, `fire_flapper`, their `_wet` variants and the
four-direction `_l` and `_r` in-hand sprites) were drawn for Civ13 (2026-11-12),
pixel by pixel from fixed palettes by `tests/sprites/create_firefighting.py`.
No borrowed or generated artwork. They are under the same terms as the rest of
Civ13's art.
