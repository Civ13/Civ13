# Wildlife validation: review candidate

The default-off `wildlife_extended` and `wildlife_production_compile` manual selectors are implemented but neither has been reviewed or run. The completed 46-vendor / nine-wildlife helper smoke at `bda4e834ae6c3fa2d65afeef65815675ae4c8bf2` is separate evidence. It does not establish full-game compilation, native species behavior or safe 96x96 movement. Production gates remain off and the 32x32 admission guard remains in place.

The extended selector compiles and runs two separate fixture worlds. It does not repeat the old 46/9 checks. The full-source fixture combines:

* Four direct `Enter` cases, 20 assertions: mammoth and T. rex, `bound_x` 0 and 64, remote `ON_BORDER` and anchor positive controls. The expected missing remote check is a gap characterization, never acceptance. Its fixture suppresses Bump and does not itself invoke native Move.
* Eighty actual single-use authorized 2px NPC Move observations, spanning both species, both bound variants, east/west, orthogonal offset 0/2, and fresh clear/remote-exit-edge/anchor controls, plus independent clear and anchor entering-phase controls. The exiting edge is selected from a measured clear move, and callback traces retain parent Bump behavior. The entering control must observe a newly occupied turf and a native Enter before its matching anchor control proves CheckExit. These observations do not bypass the production movement path or enable large actors. The remote outcome is evidence for design, not an acceptance criterion.
* Fifty-eight assertions on manually scheduled ordinary wildlife: mosquito and bat contact opportunities, recovery after valid and invalidated intent, pursuit AI ticks and attack handoff. Invalidated recovery must settle before its deadline with no pending intent or recovery owner. No forced bite or damage is introduced. These do not establish real scheduler cadence, disease, visual quality, or complete species gameplay.

The standalone native geometry fixture runs first, measuring one actor at the same anchor for both bound variants, recording readback, locs and contents, and 115 observations including fresh no-blocker controls. Its receipt is included in the evidence package when available; bounded transport can still report incomplete evidence. It characterizes engine geometry rather than predicting how bound offsets interact with physical and visual positions. Neither lane establishes that an already-overlapping placement is legal.

The separate production selector streams and verifies every tracked regular Git blob at the exact head into a fresh scratch tree, compiles the unmodified `civ13.dme`, verifies input hashes, and records outputs. It launches no game and is not a gameplay or save test. The baseline contains 5,873 files totaling 320,116,379 bytes; compiler output size and time have not been measured.

Both selectors retain the previously reviewed restricted GitHub-hosted container envelope: pinned BYOND 516.1688 and image, no container networking or credentials, read-only inputs, 2 CPUs, 3 GiB memory, 64 PIDs, 1 GiB scratch, 600-second shared deadline, and verified owned-container teardown. Each compile is capped at 120 seconds and each daemon at 90 seconds; the extended lane uses at most two compiles and two daemons, the production lane one compile and no daemons. Limit exhaustion, incomplete evidence or cleanup failure fails closed. Existing source-only tests do not prove native runtime behavior.
