# Space settlement foundations

```powershell
./tests/resource_extraction/run.ps1 -ByondBin '<pinned BYOND bin directory>' -Probe tests/space_settlement/probe.dm
```

Production sources run in the shared resource-extraction constructor fixture with ordinary game startup disabled. The probe covers phases 0 to 6 of the [space settlement roadmap](../../docs/space-settlement-roadmap.md):

- **Habitat air ledger** (`code/modules/space/habitat_ledger.dm`): sea-level fill, breathable thresholds, breathing only for the people inside while breaches and cooling run for an empty module too, capped steps so a long gap cannot replay a backlog, breathing that conserves gas, breach venting that never goes negative, cooling toward the outside, CO2 scrubbing, hatch mixing that conserves gas and weights temperature by volume, resizing, clamped fills, and a saved form of five plain numbers with no time stamps that refuses bad, missing and NaN values.
- **Barren ground and area** (`code/modules/space/barren_world.dm`): the barren area rests on regolith with a dry climate and reuses existing area art and sound; regolith and crater floor have no breathable outside air, a cold ambient temperature, no soil nutrition, no mud and no snow, and reuse the dry-dirt sprite; ordinary ground keeps breathable air and the usual climate.
- **Survey probes** (`code/modules/space/survey.dm`, phase 1): survey files with a bad schema, unknown cells or nowhere to land are refused and the shipped 40x40 map loads; the launch rail burns exactly 60 units of rocket propellant, refuses gasoline and other fuel and off-plain landing sites, and hands the probe to a console in range; terrain move costs, the survey edge, ridges, relay range and a flat battery stop the probe; each site is cored and recorded once, samples follow the terrain and the bay holds three; recharge uses a tmp clock that grants nothing after a restart and stops at full; the ascent only leaves from the landing site and drops the samples as items; the advanced probe's battery, bay and camera; a replaced survey map loses the probe; the Rocketry and Planetary Survey nodes and recipe gates; analysis turns a sample into notes on Planetary Survey. Since the review fixes: the survey record (cells seen, cored, photographed, landing sites) outlives missions and resets only with a new map; maps load by region id (bad ids refused) and match a bundle manifest by `ir_sha256`; aimed landings scatter onto nearby plains; relay dropouts and dust gusts; the advanced probe never drops commands; and notes scale by sample kind and novelty.
- **Crewed flight** (`code/modules/space/lander.dm`, phase 2): propellant mixes from hydrazine and nitric acid; only regions with a survey map can be flown to; a new launch pad waits for an admin, takes no arrivals and leads nowhere outside a region group; the crewed lander finds its pad, takes exactly 300 units of propellant and refuses gasoline, and stays grounded at an unapproved pad; travel payloads with and without a flight pass the travel decoder and malformed flights are dropped; only arriving flights build a lander, the first arrival sets it down in its survey cell and later ones join it; the 3x3 deck and its claimable bunk; the cabin starts breathable and warm with three hours of reserves for four, saves as plain numbers, does not tick with nobody connected, tops up oxygen, scrubs CO2 and holds the heat while powered, caps a long gap to one step, counts living people on the deck, and goes cold and stale without power; Crewed Flight and its recipe gates.
- **Habitat modules** (`code/modules/space/habitat_modules.dm`, phase 3): a kit unfolds a 3x3 floor inside a 16-tile hull, pressurised, breathable and warm, quarters with two bunks; modules that would not fit or overlap a floor are refused and kits snap to dock; a docked airlock gets a shared inner hatch and an outer hatch on the far side; closed hatches seal, an open hatch to a module mixes the two ledgers and is not a breach, hatch tiles share a neighbour's air; the airlock pump empties and refills the airlock losing a little air, and stops while the outer hatch is open, which vents it; a missing hull panel is a patchable breach; breathers are counted; uncabled or dry machines do nothing, the electrolyser makes oxygen from water, the scrubber strips CO2 down to its target and the heater warms; the core runs its machines each step, does not tick with nobody connected and saves its air as plain numbers; growing greenhouse plants turn CO2 into oxygen; Sealed Habitats, Life Support and their recipe gates.
- **Surface mining** (`code/modules/space/surface_mining.dm`, phase 5): drills on regolith bring up only the barren ore table, ice included; the regolith rig drills open regolith and refuses ordinary dirt, where drills keep the mainland table; an uncabled ice melter melts nothing, a powered one melts ice into water up to its capacity and then stops; the electrolyser melts ice into its own tank; the 3D printer rows for module kits, suit tanks and hull patches load with their nodes; Off-world Mining and its recipe gates.
- **Rovers** (`code/modules/space/surface_rovers.dm`, phase 5): combustion engines need air and the electric drive runs anywhere on battery charge; a sealed rover cabin starts breathable and warm and saves plain numbers; an open door is a breach; a person on the rover is in its cabin, which lets them breathe, keeps them warm and shelters them from a flare; the panel tops oxygen up on battery charge, and a flat battery leaves the cabin stale and cooling until it suffocates its crew; suit tanks empty into the oxygen reserve; a powered charger charges a parked rover and stops at full; the rover bay builds only rovers after Surface Vehicles, and the sealed template carries the drive and panel.
- **Self-sufficiency** (`code/modules/space/self_sufficiency.dm`, phase 6): an uncabled water recycler reclaims nothing; a powered one reclaims the crew's water, feeds the module's electrolyser first, keeps the rest up to its tank and gives it to a container, and an empty module reclaims none; the oxygen reclaimer scrubs CO2 to its target and returns half as oxygen; an uncabled propellant plant cracks nothing; a powered one takes ice up to its hopper, cracks one piece into propellant, does not run with nobody connected, stops when its tank is full, gives its power back while idle and fuels a lander; a mapped world can fly home to the mainland, but the mainland cannot fly to itself; flights home pass the travel decoder and payload, malformed ones are refused, and they build no lander; a trail repointed while its destination wakes sends nobody; Local Resources and the recipe gates.
- **Cargo hold and trailers** (`code/modules/space/lander.dm`, `surface_rovers.dm`): the hold takes materials and space gear but not open containers, refuses when full, packs into cargo entries and unloads onto the lander's tile; flights with cargo pass the decoder and malformed cargo (unknown kinds, zero amounts, too many entries, non-list entries, cargo without a flight) is refused; an empty hold adds nothing and a full one rides in one crew member's payload; cargo is unpacked in a crate beside its carrier, and a replayed departure removes the lander that flew it; a trailer finds a car beside it, closes up behind it after a move, comes loose when left far behind, and is gated on Surface Vehicles.
- **The Moon** (`code/modules/space/moon_world.dm`, `survey.dm`): the Moon's map settings save and allow no weather; lunar regolith is airless, colder than Mars and darker in craters, and its area is barren ground; the Moon never raises a dust storm while Mars still does; destinations and Moon terrain are named; both worlds ship survey maps; the epoch gates (the Moon from 1969, Mars from 2026) hold for probes and crewed flights; a new rail is set for the Moon, offers only open worlds, clears its aim on a change of world and will not launch to a closed one; a console files one world's survey record while flying to another and restores it on return, keeps landing sites with their world, and a probe on the Moon meets no dust.
- **Water on airless worlds** (`code/modules/space/water_scarcity.dm`): a sink elsewhere starts full and refills; on a barren world a newly built sink or well starts dry, nothing refills an older sink, an ordinary shower will not run and one left running stops, while a supplied piped shower still runs.
- **Outside exposure** (`code/modules/space/outside_exposure.dm`): a human's life tick reads the turf hooks; regolith imposes its cold and cannot be breathed without a suit, at a rate that outpaces conscious oxygen recovery; ordinary ground, no turf and the dead are untouched.
- **Pressure suits and hazards** (`code/modules/space/pressure_suits.dm`, `surface_hazards.dm`, phase 4): suit, helmet and a tank with air seal the wearer, who breathes from the tank and stays at room temperature while the heater lasts, with only insulation once it is flat; a missing helmet, empty tank or torn suit does not seal; grazes do not wear the suit, hits do, and a badly worn suit empties the tank faster; low-air warnings fire once; a patch kit is kept when there is nothing to patch; a tank refills from a module without leaving it unbreathable; suit and tank save plain numbers; cold snaps lower the surface temperature and end on their timer; flares irradiate open ground but not a module; dust storms blur sight and cut barren solar only; meteorites hole a module with a patchable breach and only strike the barren area; hazards roll at most once a minute; Pressure Suits and its recipe gates.
- **Submarine compartment air** (`code/modules/submarine/flooding_controller.dm`): each compartment holds one ledger of sea-level air sized to its deck tiles, and deck tiles carry no air of their own; nothing ticks while no player is connected, and a breached compartment keeps leaking while players are in the world even with nobody aboard; water shrinks the air pocket without losing gas and draining restores the pressure; electrolysis stops at the oxygen cap; scrubbers remove only what is there; ventilation shares air between open compartments, conserves gas and skips compartments shut off at the panel; a crew member is counted in one compartment, breathes its air, and suffocates when the oxygen is gone; a breach vents air only while the boat is surfaced (a submerged breach floods but leaves the air alone); a watertight bulkhead holds back water, one under half health passes a little, and a crumpled one passes more.
- **Submarine damage control and the thermal layer** (`code/modules/submarine/damage_control.dm`): a shoring kit cuts a breach to a seep until the next hit knocks the shoring out, wedges buckled bulkhead seals shut without mending the steel, and refuses a crumpled bulkhead or a sound hull; welding a shored breach leaves the deck dry; sonar loses half its range only across the thermal layer, so a noisy boat below it stays hidden from a frigate above while the same boat above the layer is heard.

Nothing in the game places barren ground yet (a landed lander builds its deck only in a region it is flown to), and the submarine checks use a bare crew stub on a small fixture. The survey checks cover the console logic and its window HTML, not the BYOND client, crafting in play or full-world persistence.
