# Offline sleep protection

A character who used Sleep on a fastened bed, waited out its two minutes and
then logged off cannot be harmed, stripped, operated on, grabbed, pulled or
unbuckled until the player returns. Durable Gaia is excluded. The protected
body never blocks movement or projectiles, and its bed cannot be dismantled,
unfastened or re-buckled while it is protected.

Run: `python3 tests/offline_sleep_protection/test_source.py`
