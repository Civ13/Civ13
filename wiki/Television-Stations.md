# Television stations

From 1969, build a television desk, receiver and visible studio, street or
helmet cameras with Assembly Line research and 160 crafting. TVs support live
speech, roleplay station/faction labels, skill courses and five-minute recipe
lessons under the same rules as radio.

Pair each camera by using it on its desk while held. Install and cable-power
fixed cameras, or charge a helmet camera from a power cell and clip it to worn
headgear. Choose **Video feed** at the desk and **Watch video** at a nearby,
powered, tuned receiver. Cameras must belong to that desk's paired network;
matching a frequency is insufficient. Keep cameras and receivers within 60
tiles of the desk on the same level, and stand still while watching.

Power loss, lost range, equipment movement and removed headgear end feeds.
Camera settings persist; feeds, viewers and lesson progress do not. Existing
legacy decorative televisions are retained safely, while new TV recipes make
functional receivers. Grandfather clocks are unchanged.

See [Television station operation](../docs/television-stations.md) for exact
recipes, power, batteries, controls, limits and persistence behavior.
