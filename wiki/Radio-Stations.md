# Radio stations

Radio desks, antennas and powered receivers are available from 1903. Connect
cable power, place an antenna beside the desk, and tune the desk and receivers
to the same frequency. Choose **Present live** and speak normally while next
to the desk; **Stop presenting** ends the session. Station names and optional
faction tags are roleplay labels only.

Receivers reach listeners within three tiles and must be within 60 tiles of the
station. Live broadcasts have a 32-listener cap, respect hearing and language,
and are recorded in admin logs. Existing crafting, farming and medical courses
also run on radio from 1903. Television becomes available in 1969; long-range relay construction keeps its
Modern-era requirement. See [Television stations](Television-Stations.md).

See [Radio station operation and course schedules](../docs/radio-stations.md)
for power, frequency, regional relay and skill-course details. Recipe-node lessons and television cameras have their own controls.

## Recipe lessons

On opted-in Persistence worlds, a knowledgeable live presenter can teach one recipe node in five minutes to up to 32 active listeners. Stay awake beside the same tuned receiver for the whole session. [Full requirements and limits](../docs/radio-node-lessons.md).
