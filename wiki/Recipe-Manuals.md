# Recipe manuals on Persistence worlds

On worlds that enable personal recipe knowledge, the basic recipes stay open.
Other recipes require the matching manual's knowledge as well as your faction's
research. Use a manual to study it for a minute. It remains intact, so you can
share, sell or keep it for another character. Carrying it is not required to craft.

Existing characters receive their currently unlocked knowledge once when the
feature is enabled. New characters start with basics only. Your knowledge stays
with your saved body and travels with you, but is lost on death. Keeping a manual
lets a new character learn those recipes again. Research remains the ceiling:
a manual cannot let you craft technology your faction or era has not unlocked.

Completed research produces one manual for the faction.

## Getting and sharing manuals

- Write recipe manual (IC tab): hold a pen and an unused plain sheet of paper,
  choose a node you know, then stay still for two minutes. The paper is consumed;
  the pen is reusable. Your research ceiling is checked again at completion.
- Printing press: hold an original manual in your active hand and blank paper
  in the other, then use the manual on the press. Printing takes 25 seconds,
  consumes the paper and leaves the original intact. You do not need to know
  the node to operate the press; studying its copy still needs your research.
- The provisioner's existing trading post stocks Basic Toolmaking, Primitive
  Construction and Pottery and Cookware manuals for two copper each. All posts
  of that dealer share three copies per title per hourly restock.
- Teach recipe group (IC tab): choose a nearby student and a known node. The
  student must accept. Both stay conscious, connected and together for two
  minutes. A change in location, eligibility or research cancels the lesson.
- Use one manual on another to bind them, or on a compendium to add its chapter.
  Binding takes ten seconds and consumes the source manuals. A compendium holds
  at most sixteen distinct nodes. Use it to select a chapter and study normally.
  Print individual manuals before binding; the press does not copy compendiums.

Map authors may place starter or ordinary recipe caches. Each supplies at most
one manual per active-server hour when used; there are no background item spawns.
Starter caches select era-zero Main-tree nodes, ordinary caches Main-tree nodes
up to the current world era. The saved remaining cooldown prevents reload farming.
**No caches or new trading posts are placed by this change.** Starter-cache and
ruins placement still needs a map design decision.

Manuals and compendiums remain physical items that can be traded, dropped,
stolen or destroyed. Compendiums save only bounded text IDs, not references.
Knowledge-bearing travel requires updated peers; older regions reject it rather
than silently discard your knowledge or manuals. This feature is off by default and does not
change ordinary round-map personal-knowledge requirements.

After death, a new character currently studies for the same one minute. A shorter
relearning time is not yet defined; already-known manuals need no further study.
