# New Player Guide

## Getting Started

### Read the Rules

Welcome to Civ13. Please take a moment to [read the server rules](rules/Rules.md) before joining a round.

You are welcome to ask questions and make mistakes while learning. Use mentorhelp for gameplay questions and adminhelp for rule concerns or problems. For your first two days, admins and mentors can see that you are new, so they may check in and offer help. Our [Mission and Community Standards](Mission_and_Community_Standards.md) explain the community we want to build together.

### Reporting Bugs

Found something broken? Use **Help > Report bug** in the menu bar (or the **Report Bug** verb in the Help! tab). Give a one-line summary, then describe what happened, how to make it happen again and what should have happened instead. Your BYOND key, the server, map, round and where you were standing are attached for you.

A picture of your screen is taken when you open the report. At the end you choose **Attach picture** or **No picture**. The picture shows the map around you as you saw it, without your HUD or darkness. Admins see it first and decide whether it goes on the public GitHub issue, so leave it out if it shows something you want to keep private.

An admin reviews each report. Once approved it becomes an issue on the [Civ13 GitHub](https://github.com/Civ13/Civ13/issues) for the developers, and you get the link in chat if you are online. If it is closed instead, you are told why. You can have up to three reports waiting at a time, a few minutes apart.

### Joining a Server

If you already have the BYOND client installed, skip to the server links below.

1. Download the current stable client from the [official BYOND download page](https://www.byond.com/download/) and install it. BYOND lists Windows as its player platform; its Linux download is for development and hosting, not playing. macOS is not listed as a supported player platform.
2. Open BYOND and search for **Civilization 13**. Select the game and choose **Join**, or use a direct link:
   - [RP / Nomads](byond://civ13.com:1715)
   - [TDM](byond://civ13.com:1714)
3. The game downloads its files and starts in the Lobby. Before joining a round, make sure to read the [Rules](rules/Rules.md).

#### The Civ13 launcher (Windows)

You can also play through **Civ13.exe**, the Civ13 launcher, from the [Civ13 releases page](https://github.com/Civ13/Civ13/releases) (pick the newest "Civ13 Launcher" release). It opens a window with the server list, **Join** and **Refresh** buttons, BYOND sign-in and community links.

- **Refresh** keeps servers it already found in the list and marks the ones that did not answer, instead of removing them.
- When nobody is playing, a server may be asleep. The launcher waits for it to wake up instead of reporting it as unreachable, so give it a moment.
- While you play, the launcher can show "Playing Civ13" on your Discord profile. Turn this off with **Show on Discord**.

### Your first night

Fire, shelter, water, food, warmth and people, in that order, keep a new character alive until morning. The six-line list with the first action for each is at the top of [Your First Day](guides/Guide_to_Your_First_Day.md#your-first-night) and of the [Starter Guide](Starter_Guide.md#your-first-night).

### Practice Room

To try the controls and learn how to get through your first day, choose **Practice Room** in the Lobby. You get a private room and a practice body called "Trainee". Nothing you do there affects your real character. One step at a time appears in chat.

The controls:

1. Walk onto the glowing marker with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.
2. Pick up the knife by clicking it with an empty hand.
3. Swap hands with X.
4. Pick up the straw hat and wear it.
5. Open the door in the middle wall and walk through.

Fighting and first aid:

6. Choose the red Harm intent (press 4) and hit the practice dummy.
7. Bandage the small cut on your left arm: pick up the gauze, select your left arm on the body doll, then click yourself.

Surviving your first day, in the camp behind the second door:

8. Switch back to the green Help intent (press 1) and walk into the camp. On Harm you would splash drinks and hit people.
9. Put the firewood into the campfire, then click the campfire with an empty hand to light it.
10. Put the raw meat into the lit campfire and wait for it to roast. Raw meat can give you food poisoning or worms.
11. Eat the roasted meat by clicking yourself with it.
12. Fill the pot by clicking the puddle with it. Water from puddles, rivers and wells can carry cholera.
13. Put the pot on the campfire and wait for it to boil. Boiling kills the germs.
14. Drink the boiled water by clicking yourself with the pot. Never drink straight from a puddle or river.
15. Take off your filthy, flea-ridden shirt and wash it by clicking the puddle with it. Fleas spread typhus.
16. While undressed, stand in the puddle and click yourself with the soap to wash.
17. Put your clean shirt back on.
18. Lie down in the bed by dragging your character onto it. If you fall ill, rest in a bed to recover faster and keep away from people who are coughing.
19. Get up and walk onto the exit marker.

#### Lesson board, workshop and yards

When the game sends you to the Tutorial land for your practice room, you get a whole plot of your own instead of one room: a controls room, a training room, an open camp and a drawing office, joined by doors on one line, with two large yards behind them. The steps are the same. The blue exit marker is in the south-east corner of the camp.

- **Lesson board.** The notice board beside the camp path lets you reread any topic of the in-game tutorial, from moving and the HUD to the Crafting Atlas, chat and Help. The tree, tall grass and flint in the camp are there to try the branch, twine and whittling lessons.
- **Yards.** The camp's north door opens onto a soil yard, and the office's east door onto a paved yard; the two join in the north. You can dig down and build up here just as in the live game, through every underground level and storey. Soil carries two storeys; build taller on the paving. Everything stays inside your own plot.
- **Practice stores.** The open crates in the office and in each yard hand out building materials, rail parts and tools: wood, planks, stone, bricks, glass, metals, cloth, rope, a shovel, a pickaxe, a hammer and more. Click one and choose; a full stack is put at your feet, and it never runs out.
- **Workshop desk.** Click the desk in the office and choose one exercise. Blank draughts and a pencil appear on the floor south of the desk. Draw with the ordinary designer, run its checks and save the drawing to the sheet.
  - Exercises marked **Build** or **Make** also fit out the paved yard: a stocked workshop for a car, tank or plane, a yard for an airship of any size, a rail works with sixty tiles of track for a train, a slipway and a large pool for a ship of any size the sheet allows (up to 29 tiles wide), and a works that makes rocket sections. Houses, with as many storeys as the sheet holds, and railways are pegged out in either yard and built with materials from the stores.
  - Exercises marked **Draw** or **Plan** (submarine, harbor, waterworks, contraption, space station) are drawing practice only.
  - To try another exercise, click the desk again and choose one. This clears both yards, above and below ground, including anything you built, dug or left in them, and lays out the new exercise. The lesson rooms and what you are carrying stay.

Nothing you draw, dig or build in your plot is kept, and none of it counts towards your real character or its tutorial.

When you reach the exit you return to the Lobby. To leave early, use any of these:

- Click the **click here to leave** link on any step message in chat.
- Press **Esc** and choose **Leave practice room**.
- Click the blue exit marker and confirm.
- Use **Leave Practice Room** in the Practice group at the top of the IC tab.

**Practice Instructions**, in the same group, repeats the current step. Disconnecting also ends the practice room; when you reconnect you are back in the Lobby.

If the world starts saving, the practice room closes and sends you back to the Lobby. If every room is busy, try again in a few minutes.

### In-game tutorial

The first time you join as a character, the game asks three quick questions: how movement should feel, where the name of the thing under your mouse is shown, and which HUD style you want. Pick what suits you or close a question to keep the default; all three can be changed later in **Settings** under **Controls and HUD**. Then a short tutorial walks you through the basics on your real character. Each step appears in chat and as a line at the top of the screen; click that line to see the step again. Most steps finish by themselves when you do what they ask:

1. Walk a few tiles with W, A, S and D.
2. Look at your health, food, water and stamina on the HUD.
3. Pick something up.
4. Swap hands.
5. Put an item in a pocket, belt or bag, or wear it.
6. Change your intent.
7. Open the **Crafting Atlas** (IC tab).
8. Eat or drink something.
9. Break a branch off a tree.
10. Twist crude twine from grass cuttings.
11. Sharpen a stick with a sharp flint or a knife.
12. Build and light a campfire, and stand next to it.
13. Drink from fresh water.
14. Learn the chat keys and tabs.
15. Find out where to ask for help.

Steps 9 to 13 appear on the maps where you settle and live off the land. Each one explains how in chat.

On worlds where research is written into books, one more step covers placing a book on a research bench and studying a chapter.

Use **Got it** or **Skip** in chat to move on. Your progress is saved, so the tutorial picks up where you left off in a later round. To see the current step, stop the tutorial, start it again or **Reset tutorial progress**, open the **Game Menu** with Esc and choose **Tutorial** (also the **Tutorial** verb in the OOC tab). The **Settings** page of character setup shows your tutorial status with a **Reset progress** link.

### Tips when you need them

The first time your character is dangerously cold, soaked, low on food or water, at the bottom of a dug hole, knocked down or holding a crafting material, one blue **Tip** line in chat says what to do. Each tip is shown once. To turn tips off, switch off **Show tips** on the Settings tab of Character Setup.

### Your first round

Use the [Game Modes Overview](gamemodes/Game_Modes.md) to find your scenario's first steps and detailed guide.

Read the lobby's map and mode before choosing a role. In Nomads, start with the [Starter Guide](Starter_Guide.md): secure water, food and shelter before advancing production. On persistent worlds such as North and South America you must study a chapter from a book before you can craft it; [Your First Day](guides/Guide_to_Your_First_Day.md) shows how to get a book and get through the first day. Use [Character & Skills](guides/Guide_to_Character.md) when an interaction says you lack expertise.

Crafting depends on the active map's catalog, era, character skills and research mode. A recipe listed here may be unavailable on your map. For the tech-tree mode, read [Research](guides/Guide_to_Research.md); older modes have separate rules in [Civilizations and Nomads](gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#research).

For later production, see [Chemistry](guides/Guide_to_Chemistry.md), [Power & Batteries](guides/Guide_to_Power.md), and [Crafting](guides/Guide_to_Crafting.md).

Use [Farming](guides/Guide_to_Farming.md) for seeds and irrigation, [Medical](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md#severe-illness) for illness treatment, [Winter & Long Rounds](guides/Guide_to_Survival.md) for cold protection and debris storage, and [Resource Extraction](guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md) for gathering and powered production.
