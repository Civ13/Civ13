# Antarctica

Antarctica is a land of the persistent worlds drawn after the frozen continent around the southern pole: ice, a long mountain chain and a cold sea full of drifting ice. It is the hardest land to live in. Nothing grows on the ice and its fresh water is frozen solid, so food has to come from the sea, the hunt or a ship's hold. Those who make it there can work a seam of coal far from anyone else.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Climate | Ice and tundra everywhere, with a few green islands far offshore |
| Local time | The reference time for the other lands; southern seasons, so its summer is the north's winter |
| Rich in | Coal |
| Peoples | None of its own |
| Neighbours | None on foot; every other land lies far to the north across the sea |
| Coasts | Every side |

## Getting there

Antarctica is surrounded by sea: no walk leads there.

- **By ship.** Sail from any other land's coast and choose Antarctica from the voyage list. It is a long voyage, so pack rations for everyone aboard (see [Sailing between the continents](../guides/Guide_to_Boats.md#sailing-between-the-continents) and [Rations and the sea](../guides/Guide_to_Boats.md#rations-and-the-sea)).
- **By plane.** Fly from any other land, with a full tank (see [Flying to another continent](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md#flying-to-another-continent)).

## The land

- **The ice** is deadly cold all year, and blizzards make it worse. Furs, a fire and a roof are not optional (see [Winter & Long Rounds](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md)).
- **The mountains** run across the continent and along the peninsula. Mined-out mountain floor keeps giving stone and regrowing ore seams (see [Old mountain floor and ore seams](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#old-mountain-floor-and-ore-seams)).
- **Food is the hard part.** Lakes and rivers are frozen, so no crop has water to grow, and only the fringes give seeds when you forage. Fish, hunt and bring supplies by sea.
- **No diving.** There is no seabed level under the sea, so submarines stay on the surface, and aircraft carriers and freighters with a hold below the waterline need [Gaia](../maps/Gaia.md).

## Resources

- **Coal** turns up twice as often as usual.
- **Iron** is found at the usual rate; other ores are scarcer than usual (see [Each continent's ores](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#each-continents-ores)).
- **Oil:** springs on land run dry here. Drill offshore instead (see [Offshore drilling rigs](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#offshore-drilling-rigs)).

## Peoples and languages

Antarctica has no peoples of its own. Characters who arrive keep the people and language they were born with (see [Peoples and Languages](../guides/Guide_to_Peoples_and_Languages.md)).
