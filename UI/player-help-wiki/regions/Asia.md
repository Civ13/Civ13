# Asia

Asia is the largest land of the persistent worlds, drawn after the real continent from the Urals to the far north-east cape: the frozen north, the steppe, the deserts and high plateau of the middle, and the monsoon coasts and jungles of the south and east. It borders three other lands on foot, and its mines hold the saltpeter, silver and gemstones others need.

## Getting there

Asia's west edge joins two lands: its northern part leads to Europe and its southern part to Africa. Its south edge, among the southern islands, leads to Oceania.

- **On foot.** Walk off Europe's east edge, Africa's east edge beyond the desert peninsula, or Oceania's north edge, and you come out on the matching edge of Asia (see [Walking to a neighbouring land](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#walking-to-a-neighbouring-land)).
- **By ship.** Asia's coasts face the open sea to the east, south and north (see [Sailing between the continents](../guides/Guide_to_Boats.md#sailing-between-the-continents)).
- **By plane.** Fly from any other land (see [Flying to another continent](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md#flying-to-another-continent)).

## The land

- **The north** is a vast belt of taiga with tundra and ice along the Arctic coast.
- **The middle** is open steppe, sandy deserts and the highest mountains of the world, with a high, cold plateau among them.
- **The east** is temperate and well watered, with long rivers running down to the sea and islands off the coast.
- **The south and south-east** are hot savanna and monsoon jungle, broken into peninsulas and island chains.
- **Mountains** are everywhere in the middle. Mined-out mountain floor keeps giving stone and regrowing ore seams (see [Old mountain floor and ore seams](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#old-mountain-floor-and-ore-seams)).
- **No diving.** There is no seabed level under the sea, so submarines stay on the surface, and aircraft carriers and freighters with a hold below the waterline need [Gaia](../maps/Gaia.md).

## Resources

- **Saltpeter, silver, gemstones and diamonds** turn up twice as often as usual. Saltpeter makes Asia the place for gunpowder.
- **Cinnabar** is twice as common (see [Mercury](../guides/Guide_to_Mercury.md)), and the rivers and coasts give more **black iron sand** than other lands (see [Tamahagane](../guides/Guide_to_Metallurgy.md#tamahagane-steel-from-iron-sand)).
- **Silk:** Asia is the only one of Earth's lands where broadleaf trees are mulberries, so silk is raised here (see [Resins, Silk & Fabrics](../guides/Guide_to_Resins_and_Fabrics.md)).
- **Rubber:** every living jungle tree is a rubber tree (see [Rubber trees](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#rubber-trees)).
- **Iron** is found at the usual rate; other ores are scarcer than usual. Springs of oil on land run dry here, so drill offshore (see [Each continent's ores](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#each-continents-ores)).

## Peoples and languages

Someone born in Asia belongs to the people of the climate they were born in: **Mongols** (Mongolian) in the tundra, **Koreans** (Korean) in the taiga, **Han** (Chinese) in the temperate and dry lands, **Persians** (Farsi) in the desert, **Yamato** (Japanese) on the coasts and **Viet** (Vietnamese) in the jungle and savanna. See [Peoples and Languages](../guides/Guide_to_Peoples_and_Languages.md).
