# Greenland

Greenland is a land of the persistent worlds drawn after the great ice island of the north, with green fjords along its southern coasts. Iceland lies on the same map. It is hard country: most of it is ice, food is scarce, and the cold never lets up for long. Its reward is in the rock, which is rich in calamine, lead and gold.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Climate | Tundra and ice over most of the island, taiga in patches and along the southern fjords |
| Local time | 3 hours behind the reference time; northern seasons, with the sun low in the sky |
| Rich in | Calamine (zinc), lead and gold |
| Peoples | Inuit |
| Neighbours | None on foot; Gaia lies to the south across the sea |
| Coasts | Every side |

## Getting there

Greenland is an island: no walk leads there.

- **By ship.** Sail from any other land's coast and choose Greenland from the voyage list (see [Sailing between the continents](../guides/Guide_to_Boats.md#sailing-between-the-continents)). Pack ice drifts off its coasts.
- **By plane.** Fly from any other land (see [Flying to another continent](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md#flying-to-another-continent)).

## The land

- **The ice** covers almost all of the island. It is bitterly cold all year, and colder still on high ground and in a blizzard. Dress in furs, keep a fire and build out of the wind (see [Winter & Long Rounds](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md)).
- **The southern fjords and Iceland** are the greenest places, with patches of taiga.
- **Mountains** line the east coast. Mined-out mountain floor keeps giving stone and regrowing ore seams (see [Old mountain floor and ore seams](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#old-mountain-floor-and-ore-seams)).
- **Farming is hard.** Most of the farmable ground lies under snow on the tundra coasts; clear it with a spade before you plant, and expect short seasons (see [Farming](../guides/Guide_to_Farming.md)). Fishing and hunting feed most people here.
- **No diving.** There is no seabed level under the sea, so submarines stay on the surface, and aircraft carriers and freighters with a hold below the waterline need [Gaia](../maps/Gaia.md).

## Resources

- **Calamine, lead and gold** turn up twice as often as usual.
- **Iron** is found at the usual rate; other ores are scarcer than usual (see [Each continent's ores](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#each-continents-ores)).
- **Oil:** springs on land run dry here. Drill offshore instead (see [Offshore drilling rigs](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#offshore-drilling-rigs)).

## Peoples and languages

Everyone born in Greenland is **Inuit**, with Inuktitut as their mother tongue. See [Peoples and Languages](../guides/Guide_to_Peoples_and_Languages.md).
