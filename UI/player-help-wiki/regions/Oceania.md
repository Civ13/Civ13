# Oceania

Oceania is a land of the persistent worlds drawn after the island continent of the south, the great islands north of it and the island chains of the warm seas. It is the flattest of the lands, with a dry heart and green coasts, and the only place on Earth rich in uranium.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Climate | Desert and dry scrub inland, savanna in the north, temperate in the south-east and on the southern islands, jungle on the islands to the north |
| Local time | 9 hours ahead of the reference time; southern seasons, so its summer is the north's winter |
| Rich in | Bauxite, uranium and gemstones |
| Peoples | None of its own yet |
| Neighbours | Asia on foot from the northern islands; the other lands by sea or air |
| Coasts | Every side |

## Getting there

- **On foot from Asia.** Walk off the south edge of Asia among its southern islands and you come out on Oceania's north edge. Walk off Oceania's north edge to go back (see [Walking to a neighbouring land](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#walking-to-a-neighbouring-land)). The islands are separated by water, so plan the crossing where the land carries on.
- **By ship.** The sea lies all round Oceania (see [Sailing between the continents](../guides/Guide_to_Boats.md#sailing-between-the-continents)).
- **By plane.** Fly from any other land (see [Flying to another continent](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md#flying-to-another-continent)).

## The land

- **The heart of the continent** is sandy desert ringed by dry scrub. Water is scarce far from the coast.
- **The north** is hot savanna, and the great islands north of the continent are mountainous jungle.
- **The east** has a long mountain range near the coast, with the wettest, greenest land between it and the sea.
- **The south-east and the islands beyond it** are temperate and mild.
- **Flat country.** Oceania is the flattest of the lands, so travel overland is easy, but there are fewer mountains to mine.
- **No diving.** There is no seabed level under the sea, so submarines stay on the surface, and aircraft carriers and freighters with a hold below the waterline need [Gaia](../maps/Gaia.md).

## Resources

- **Bauxite, uranium and gemstones** turn up twice as often as usual. Oceania is the place to look for the ores of the late ages: aluminium (see [Crafting](../guides/Guide_to_Crafting.md)) and uranium (see [Radiation](../guides/Guide_to_Radiation.md)).
- **Iron** is found at the usual rate; other ores are scarcer than usual (see [Each continent's ores](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#each-continents-ores)).
- **Oil:** springs on land run dry here. Drill offshore instead (see [Offshore drilling rigs](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#offshore-drilling-rigs)).
- **No rubber trees** and no mulberries for silk.

## Peoples and languages

Oceania has no peoples of its own yet. Characters who arrive keep the people and language they were born with (see [Peoples and Languages](../guides/Guide_to_Peoples_and_Languages.md)).
