# Regions of the World

The persistent worlds are split into **regions**: separate worlds, each on its own server, that together make up one Earth with Gaia in its Atlantic. Your character lives in one region at a time and can travel between them on foot, by ship, by plane, by rocket or by trail. Every region keeps its own research, its own era and its own factions, so a land that is still in the Stone Age can sit beside one that is building aircraft.

This page lists every region, how to reach it and what makes it different. Each land has a page of its own, linked from the table.

```admonish info
Open **OOC > Regions** (or press **Esc** and choose **Regions**) in game to see every region, whether it is up, asleep or stopped, and how many people are there. See [Travel Between Lands](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md) for the rules that apply to every trip.
```

## The lands

| Region | What it is | Climate | Rich in | Getting there |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Gaia](../maps/Gaia.md) | The mainland. New characters start here. A land of several continents with a seabed, an underground and the fastest-growing crops. | Every climate, from tundra to jungle | Copper | You start here. Ship or plane from any other land. |
| [North America](../maps/The_Americas.md) | A northern continent from the polar ice to the isthmus. | Tundra and taiga in the north, temperate forest and plains, dry west, jungle in the south | Iron and lead | Ship or plane, or walk north from South America. |
| [South America](../maps/The_Americas.md) | A southern continent from the isthmus to the cold cape. | Jungle basin, savanna, a long mountain chain, cold south | Gold, silver and bauxite | Ship or plane, or walk south from North America. |
| [Europe](Europe.md) | Europe from the Atlantic shore to the Urals and the northern ice. | Tundra and taiga in the north, temperate in the middle, steppe in the south-east | Coal and calamine (zinc) | Ship or plane, or walk west from Asia. |
| [Africa](Africa.md) | Africa from the great desert to the southern cape, with the desert peninsula to its east. | Desert, savanna and jungle across the equator, temperate at the cape | Sulphur and tin | Ship or plane, or walk from Asia. |
| [Asia](Asia.md) | Asia from the Urals to the far north-east cape: the steppe, the high plateau and the monsoon coasts. | Every climate: tundra, taiga, steppe, desert, temperate, savanna and jungle | Saltpeter, silver and gemstones | Ship or plane, or walk from Europe, Africa or Oceania. |
| [Oceania](Oceania.md) | The island continent of the south, the great islands north of it and the island chains of the warm seas. | Desert heart, savanna north, temperate south-east, jungle islands | Bauxite, uranium and gemstones | Ship or plane, or walk from Asia's southern islands. |
| [Greenland](Greenland.md) | The great ice island of the north, with green fjords along its southern coasts. | Tundra and taiga | Calamine (zinc), lead and gold | Ship or plane only. |

"Rich in" means those ores turn up **twice as often** there; most other ores are scarcer than usual, and iron is found at the usual rate everywhere (see [Each continent's ores](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#each-continents-ores)). **Faction > Province Holdings** shows what the land you are in is rich in.

## Where the lands lie

Gaia lies in the Atlantic, east of the Americas and west of Europe and Africa, with Greenland to its north. Asia lies east of Europe, Oceania to the south-east of Asia, and Antarctica far to the south of everything.

Where one land carries on into the next you can **walk across the map edge** (see [Walking to a neighbouring land](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#walking-to-a-neighbouring-land)):

| From | Walk off the | Into |
| --- | --- | --- |
| North America | south edge, at the isthmus | South America |
| Europe | east edge | Asia, northern part of its west edge |
| Africa | east edge, beyond the desert peninsula | Asia, southern part of its west edge |
| Asia | south edge, among the southern islands | Oceania, north edge |

Every other trip crosses the sea. A ship you built can sail to any land with a coast (see [Sailing between the continents](../guides/Guide_to_Boats.md#sailing-between-the-continents)), and a plane can fly to any land (see [Flying to another continent](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md#flying-to-another-continent)). Gaia, Greenland and Antarctica are islands and can only be reached that way.

## What every land shares

- **One clock and calendar.** The lands share one calendar, but each keeps its own local time, and the southern lands have their seasons the other way round (see [Time zones and seasons across the lands](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md#time-zones-and-seasons-across-the-lands)).
- **One sky.** Weather systems drift from one land to the next (see [Weather & Climate](../guides/Guide_to_Weather.md)).
- **Climate zones.** Each land is made of climate zones, and the zone sets the temperature, the crops that grow, the wild plants and the animals (see [How cold it gets](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md#how-cold-it-gets)).
- **Hills and rivers.** Every land has hills that slow you climbing, and rivers that flood after long rain and in the spring thaw (see [Hills](../maps/The_Americas.md#hills) and [Rivers & Floods](../guides/Guide_to_Floods.md)).
- **Four levels underground** of solid rock that opens as you dig, richer the deeper you go (see [Digging down](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#digging-down)).
- **Room to build upward,** with an air level above the land for planes (see [Construction](../guides/Guide_to_Construction.md) and [Aircraft](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md)).
- **Provinces.** Every land is divided into provinces that factions can hold, and two or three of them are rich in one of the land's own ores (see [Provinces](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#provinces) and [Rich provinces](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md#rich-provinces)).
- **Peoples.** A character belongs to a people of the land they were born in, and the people gives their mother tongue (see [Peoples and Languages](../guides/Guide_to_Peoples_and_Languages.md)).
- **Shared OOC.** OOC chat reaches every land; what you say in character stays in your own land (see [Talking to other lands](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#talking-to-other-lands)).

## Your home land

- **New characters start on Gaia,** near other players if you are new to the game.
- **You live where you travel.** Your home moves with your character, and joining the game takes you straight to the land your character lives in.
- **If you die,** your next character starts beside your bed in the land where you died, if you have one there. Without a bed, you start again on Gaia.
- **Starting elsewhere.** Once a land has been settled, members of a faction with a colony there can start a new character at the colony (see [Factions and colonies](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#factions-and-colonies)).

## Worlds beyond the lands

| World | What it is | Getting there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Orbit** | Party space stations above the Earth. | By rocket from a launch pad, from 1969. See [Space Stations](../guides/Guide_to_Space_Stations.md). |
| **The Moon** | A grey, airless world: no weather, -100 C in the open, ice in the shadowed craters. Live in sealed habitats. | By crewed lander from a launch pad, from 1969. See [The Moon & Mars](../guides/Guide_to_the_Moon.md). |
| **Mars** | Thin, unbreathable air at -60 C, with dust storms. Live in sealed habitats. | By crewed lander from a launch pad, from 2026. See [The Moon & Mars](../guides/Guide_to_the_Moon.md). |
| **Rail Yard** | Each party's own train berth, with sleeper beds and freight. | Your party's train. See [Party trains](../guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#party-trains). |

No trail leads off the Earth: the Moon, Mars and orbit are reached only by rocket, and a flight home always lands on Gaia.

## The way in

Two small worlds help you get into the game. Nobody lives in either, and nothing you do there is saved.

- **The gateway** is the server you join from the hub. It sends you straight on to the land your character lives in, or to Gaia if you have no character yet. If your land is restarting, the gateway keeps trying for a couple of minutes, then gives you a **Try again** link. While you wait it can offer you the practice rooms.
- **The tutorial** holds the [practice rooms](../New_Player_Guide.md#practice-room). Choose **Practice Room** in the lobby to get a private room and a practice body; when you leave, you are sent back on to your land.
