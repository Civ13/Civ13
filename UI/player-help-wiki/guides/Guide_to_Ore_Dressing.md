# Ore Dressing

Most gold and silver is not found as clean ore. It is locked in rock, and the rock has to be crushed and washed before the metal comes out. This is called ore dressing.

## Lode ore

<img src="../images/Gold-bearing_quartz.png"> From the **Classical age** on, mining rock now and then gives four lumps of lode ore, on top of whatever else the rock gives:

- **Gold-bearing quartz** is white rock with rusty stains and a few specks of gold.
- **Silver-bearing ore** is heavy, dark lead ore with bright grey glints.

Lode ore turns up far more often than clean gold or silver ore, but each lump holds only a little metal. Keep it, crush it, and wash it.

## Crushing

Each lump of lode ore becomes one load of crushed ore. You can crush it in:

- A **stone crusher**: use the ore on it, one lump at a time.
- <img src="../images/Water_stamp_mill.png"> A **water stamp mill** (12 wood, **Ore Dressing**). Set it next to a turning mill wheel, treadwheel, horse gin or windmill and fasten it with a wrench. Load up to fifty lumps or stone blocks into it, then use it with an empty hand to start it. It crushes one every six seconds while something drives it.
- A **powered stamp mill** (Electricity), which works the same way on cable power and crushes one every four seconds.

Crushed ore collects under the crusher or mill. Clear it before twelve stacks pile up.

## Washing

Each load of crushed ore holds about half a lump of metal. Washing gets some of it out, and the rest goes out with the waste, called **tailings**. Every load you wash leaves a load of tailings.

| Method | How | Metal kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pan | Hold crushed ore and use it on a wooden bowl, beside shallow fresh water. | About a fifth |
| Buddle | Use crushed ore on a buddle that has water in it. | About a third |

<img src="../images/Buddle.png"> A **buddle** (6 wood, **Ore Dressing**) is a round pit with a turning sweep. Build it beside water, or pour water into it with a bucket. You keep washing load after load until the crushed ore or the water runs out, or you step away. To take it apart, hit it with a hammer on harm intent.
