# Guide to Drugs and Stimulants

Tobacco, opium poppies, coca, cannabis and khat each grow in their own lands. People who can grow a crop are often far from the people who want it, so they are worth trading. Every drug has a real use: painkilling for wounds and surgery, stamina for long work, and mood for morale. Every drug can also hurt you. Habits form, doses that were safe stop working, and too much at once can kill.

Hemp and cannabis are separate seeds. Hemp gives fibre for rope and cloth. Cannabis gives the flowering tops. See the [Guide to Farming](Guide_to_Farming.md) for planting, water and seasons.

## Traditional preparations

These need no research beyond what the step names.

- **Tobacco.** Dry the leaves on a drying rack. Dried tobacco is chewed (click yourself with it), smoked in a pipe, or rolled into cigarettes. To roll cigarettes, click 2 dried tobacco with a blank sheet of paper. That makes 2 cigarettes.
- **Opium.** When a poppy is ripe, hold a knife on help intent and click it to score the pods. Wait about five minutes for the latex to dry, then pick it off by hand as a ball of raw opium. A well-fed plant gives two balls. Each poppy can be lanced three times, and you can still harvest it afterwards. Smoke opium in a pipe or swallow it.
- **Poppy tea.** Grind poppy heads in a mortar into poppy straw. Mix 1 part straw with 2 parts water for poppy tea, a weak and unreliable opiate.
- **Coca.** Chew the leaves by clicking yourself with them. Holding wood ash in your other hand frees more from each wad. Ground coca from a mortar, mixed 1 to 2 with water, makes coca tea.
- **Cannabis.** Dried tops smoke in a pipe or roll as a joint from 2 dried tops. Fresh tops rub into a lump of hashish (5 fresh tops, with Herbalism). Hashish is smoked in a pipe or eaten.
- **Khat.** Eat the fresh shoots. They wilt within about an hour of picking, so khat is a local trade or a job for a fast courier.

## Apothecary and refined drugs

- **Laudanum.** Dissolve a ball of opium in at least 10 units of spirits or wine, which is 1 part opium to 2 parts drink. Laudanum is a measured liquid painkiller for doctors. It has alcohol in it, so a drink on top of a dose adds up fast.
- **Refining.** A **laboratory distiller** (Apothecary) refines the main ingredient in it, if the person who starts it knows the right research and has a trained medic's skill. Put an empty beaker in as the collector, pour the mixture in, then start it.
  - Opium becomes **morphine** (Pharmacology). Two units of opium give one of morphine.
  - Ground coca becomes **cocaine** (Pharmacology). Two units of coca give one of cocaine.
  - Morphine becomes **heroin** (Modern Medicine).
  - If you don't know the research, the distiller keeps the mixture and nothing comes of it.
- **Purity.** How pure a batch comes out depends on the skill of the person running the distiller. A skilled doctor gets a clean batch. A barely trained one gets up to half plant residue, which does nothing.

## Packaging and testing

Click a container with a blank sheet of paper to fold up to 10 units of it into a **paper twist**. A twist looks the same whatever is in it, and a seller can cut a drug with any filler, such as sugar or flour. To check one, right-click it and choose **Taste a Pinch**. That tells you whether it is strong and clean, weak, or mostly filler, but you swallow a little. A pill maker shows exactly what is inside.

A buyer used to cut powder who gets a pure twist can take far more than they meant to.

## Effects

| Drug | What it does for you | What it costs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tobacco | Mood and calm | Strong habit; heavy smoking slowly damages the lungs |
| Coca leaf and tea | Stamina, eases hunger | Mild habit |
| Khat | Stamina and mood | Poor sleep, mild habit, jitters if you take too much |
| Cocaine | Large stamina boost, pain partly ignored, faster movement, mood | Very addictive; an overdose drives the heart until it fails |
| Crack | As cocaine, stronger and shorter | Lower overdose point, faster habit, brain damage on overdose |
| Cannabis and hashish | Mood, mild painkiller | A strong dose brings panic and hallucinations |
| Opium and poppy tea | Strong painkiller, sleep | Drowsy; an overdose slows breathing until it stops |
| Laudanum | Opium's painkilling in a measured dose | As opium, and it already has alcohol in it |
| Morphine | The strongest painkiller before modern medicine | Very addictive; breathing stops at a low overdose |
| Heroin | The strongest painkilling and high | The lowest overdose point and the fastest habit |
| Coffee | Stamina | A mild habit; withdrawal is a headache |

### Dangers

- **Mixing.** Any opiate taken with alcohol slows breathing at a far lower dose. Two stimulants together strain the heart.
- **Tolerance and relapse.** If you use a drug regularly, each dose does less and you can take more before it is an overdose. Tolerance fades faster than the craving does. So someone who comes back after a break and takes their old dose can overdose.
- **Habits.** Each kind of drug builds its own habit. Any drug of the same kind eases the craving, so morphine eases an opium habit. Withdrawal is miserable, with pain, shaking, poor sleep and low mood, but only heavy drinking withdrawal can kill.
- **Lasting harm.** A heavy habit over a long time slowly damages one organ: the lungs for tobacco, the heart for stimulants and the liver for alcohol.
- **Death is real.** A death from an overdose or a mix is an ordinary death.

### Signs others can see

When you examine someone, you may see pinpoint pupils and drowsiness (opiates), wide pupils and restlessness (stimulants), red, heavy eyes (cannabis), the smell of tobacco smoke, or shaking hands (a heavy user in withdrawal). Doctors and courts can act on what they see.

## Medicine

Opium, laudanum and morphine are the main painkillers for wounds and surgery before modern medicine. See the [Guide to Medical](Guide_to_Medical.md). A doctor who measures doses carefully gets the benefit without the habit, which gives poppy farmers honest buyers.

## Faction rules

On a faction's registered land, the faction leader can set each kind of drug to allowed, licensed or banned. The kinds are opiates, stimulants, tobacco, cannabis and alcohol. Use **Faction Papers** and choose **Set substance rules on our land**. Everything is allowed until a rule is set.

- **Examine** an item on that land to see whether it is banned there or needs a licence.
- **Read the laws** in Faction Justice lists the rules after the written laws.
- A **substance licence** is a faction paper, issued and revoked like a trading licence. Members of the faction never need one.
- **Police dogs** on faction land bark at whatever that faction bans.

The game only shows the rules. The faction enforces them with its own warrants, trials, fines and jail. See [Paperwork](Guide_to_Paperwork.md).
