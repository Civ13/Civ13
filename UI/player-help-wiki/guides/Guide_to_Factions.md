# Guide to Factions

A faction is your team: its members share research, land, a treasury and a name. This guide walks through a faction's whole life, from founding it to holding provinces, claiming an island across the sea, joining a nation and going to war. It is written for new members and for leaders alike.

```admonish info
Factions you found yourself exist on [Nomads](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md) maps and in the connected lands of the wider world (see [Regions](../regions/Regions.md)). Provinces, nations, islands and sieges belong to the wider world only. RP maps come with preset factions and may switch some of this off. The **Faction tab** only shows what the current map allows.
```

The full tables for halls, governments, settlement ranks and sieges are on the [Civilizations and Nomads](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#factions) page. This guide explains how the parts fit together and what to do, step by step.

## Why join a faction

- **Research.** Only faction members research, and everyone in a faction shares its research. A nomad can only craft what the land already knows.
- **Land.** A faction can register plots, hold provinces and claim an island of its own.
- **Safety.** Members asleep in their own beds in land their faction holds cannot be harmed while they are logged off (see [Sleeping safely](#sleeping-safely)).
- **Starting with friends.** New characters can start beside the faction's recruitment station instead of alone.
- **People.** Shared stores, workshops, a treasury that pays wages, and someone to watch your back.

## Founding a faction

1. Open the **Faction tab** and choose **Create Faction**. You must not already be in one.
2. Give it a **name** (it must be new), an optional **motto** (up to 80 characters) and optional **flavour text** (up to 120 words). Players see these in **Check Faction List**.
3. Draw its **symbol** on the 32x32 canvas, or stamp a classic shape and draw over it. The banner colours come from the drawing. **Copy design code** saves the symbol as text so you can reuse it elsewhere.

You become its **Leader**. One player can lead only one faction at a time, even across several characters. You can later rename it (**Rename Faction**; the old name is shown for the next 8 seasons) or redraw the symbol (**Modify Faction Symbol**).

A new faction holds **10 members**. Build a [faction hall](#the-faction-hall) to hold more.

## Joining and leaving

**Joining.** There are two ways in:

- **Be recruited:** the leader, or a member with the **Recruitment** permission, stands beside you, right-clicks you and chooses **Recruit**. If you are already in another faction, accepting moves you over as an ordinary member, without the rank or permissions you had before.
- **Use a recruitment station:** click the faction's station to read its rules, then **Join** at once or **Apply** with a short introduction. You must be factionless to apply. When an application is approved, come back to any of its stations and **Confirm and join**. Applications and approved offers expire after 7 days, and dying ends that character's application.

A full faction cannot take new members, but anyone already counted among its members can always rejoin. Your place belongs to your account, so it survives your character's death. It is freed when you leave, or after two weeks without playing a member.

**Leaving.** **Abandon Faction** on the **Faction tab** makes you a nomad again. You cannot rejoin the same faction for **24 hours**, and others can see that you are a former member. If the leader leaves, the seat stands empty. Leaving also ends your residency for [province stake](#provinces-holding-land-on-a-continent), so a member who leaves and rejoins starts the 3-day count again.

Nobody can be thrown out of a faction by a button. A faction that wants someone gone has to persuade them, or use its own [laws and courts](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#laws-and-courts).

## Ranks: leader, officers and offices

A faction has three layers of authority.

**The leader** can do everything: recruit, set the member limit (**Set Member Limit**), set taxes and wages, create offices, designate fixtures, write laws, and choose the [defence window](#war-and-sieges). **Transfer Faction Leadership** hands the seat to a member standing nearby. If the seat is empty, any living member can take it with **Become Faction Leader**. Members can also call a leadership election if the leader has not played for more than 3 days or has served past the faction's term.

**Permissions** are small grants the leader gives with **Faction Perms** (right-click a member):

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Recruitment** | Recruit people and review station applications. It gives no treasury, tax or permission powers. Only the leader grants it. |
| **Announcements** | Send a **Faction Announcement** from the **Officer tab** to every member. |
| **Giving Titles** | Give and remove titles with **Give Faction Title** on the **Officer tab**. Titles are names only. |
| **Permission Management** | Grant or remove Announcements and Giving Titles. Only the leader grants it. |

**Offices** are named posts with real powers, set up in **Faction Offices**. Every faction starts with a **Treasurer** and a **Registrar**, and the leader can add up to 12 more, such as Judge, Herald or Diplomat. Each office gets any of these powers:

| Power | Lets the holder |
| --- | --- |
| Treasury | Open the strongbox, collect taxes, draw bills and notes |
| Land register | Register and transfer plots and deeds |
| Seals | Cut and revoke faction seals |
| Coin dies | Cut the dies for the faction's coins |
| Justice | Hold trials, issue warrants and pardons, set cell timers |
| Papers | Issue citizenship papers, travel passes, permits and licences; record name changes; keep the island border and grant visitor passes |
| Diplomacy | Draft and sign treaties between factions |
| Hall and fixtures | Fasten, unfasten and move the hall, stations, charter and strongbox |

The leader appoints a member for a set number of days or with no limit. A **faction seal** cut for an office carries its powers: hand the seal over to lend the office, and revoke it to take it back. Offices can also be filled by a 30-minute **election**. See [Offices and elections](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#offices-and-elections).

When this guide says **officer**, it means someone holding an office with the power in question.

## Papers and permits

**Faction Papers** on the **Faction tab** keeps the census and the papers. Anyone with the **papers** power issues papers to a person standing beside them, using a blank sheet of paper:

- **Citizenship papers** last until revoked.
- A **travel pass** lasts 1 to 30 days.
- A **building permit** or **trading licence** lasts 1 to 30 days, or until revoked.

Papers name their holder, so stolen or copied papers open nothing. They count only when held in your hands or worn, not packed in a bag. Faction doors can be set to open for members only, members and citizens, or members, citizens and pass holders. The papers power can also **Require permits on our land**, so outsiders need a building permit to build on the faction's plots or a trading licence to set up a stall there. Members and deed holders never need one. Details are in [Census and papers](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#census-and-papers).

## The faction hall

The hall decides how many members a faction can have. Craft a **faction hall seat** from 20 wood planks (**economy & production**), place it, and the leader clicks it and chooses **Designate**. Members then hand it materials to raise it a stage at a time:

| Stage | Members | Era |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Council fire | 15 | Stone Age |
| Longhouse | 25 | Classical Age, under a roof |
| Town hall | 35 | Medieval Age, under a roof |
| Capitol | 50 | Industrial Age, under a roof |

Examine the hall to see what the next stage needs. Each standing [colony](#colonies-in-other-lands) adds room for 5 more members, and your side's best ranked settlement adds 3 per rank (see [Settlements](#settlements-camp-to-city)). If the hall is destroyed, the faction drops back to 10 new places but nobody is removed.

To move the hall, the leader or an officer with the hall and fixtures power right-clicks it, chooses **Fasten or Unfasten**, pulls it to its new place and fastens it again. A loose hall counts for nothing and anyone can drag it away, so fasten it quickly. The same works for recruitment stations, the nation assembly, the colony charter and the strongbox.

## Recruitment stations

A **recruitment station** (10 planks, **economy & production**) lets players join without a recruiter online. The leader clicks it and chooses **Designate for my faction**. Only the leader can release it again.

- **Rules (leader only):** **Closed** stops new applications. **Review every application** sends every application to the queue (new stations start here). **Accept matching applicants at once** lets players who meet the rules join straight away.
- **Requirements** can ask for a language and minimum skill levels (0-10) in crafting, farming, medical and combat. **Open recruitment** sets them aside.
- **Reviewing:** the leader and recruiters approve, reject or revoke applications at any station of the faction, or from anywhere with **Recruitment Applications** on the **Faction tab**.
- **Starting here:** members' new characters can choose to start **with** the faction, beside one of its working stations, already a member. You must have joined the faction in play at least once.

On a continent, a station only works in a province its side holds, with at least 3 of the faction's own residents there (see below). An idle station keeps its rules and applications, and examining it says why it is idle. Stations on lands across the sea, which have no provinces, always work.

## Money: the strongbox and taxes

A faction's money is real coin in a **strongbox** (10 wood, needs Trade & Coinage). The leader uses it with an empty hand and chooses **Make it the treasury**: it bolts down and locks. Only the leader and treasurers can open it. Outsiders can still pry it open with a crowbar or hammer, which takes a minute and warns every member online.

- **Taxes:** the leader sets a **sales tax** on players' vending sales and a **business tax** on company profits, each 0-30%. Taxes build up as people trade, and a treasurer turns them into coin with **Collect taxes into the box**.
- **Wages:** up to 50 silver a day per member, paid only from coin actually in the box. Unpaid wages stop growing after 7 days.
- **Dues:** up to 200 silver a week. Members pay by holding coins and choosing **Pay dues from my hand**. Nobody is forced to pay.
- **Bills and notes:** treasurers can write bills of exchange (up to 500 silver) or print batches of treasury notes that anyone can cash at the box.
- **Upkeep and tribute** for settlements and vassals come out of this box, so keep it stocked.

The full rules are in [Faction treasury](Guide_to_Money.md#faction-treasury). Plots and deeds are in [Land and deeds](Guide_to_Money.md#land-and-deeds).

## Provinces: holding land on a continent

Each continent is cut into **provinces** along coasts, rivers, lakes, mountain ranges and straits. **Province Holdings** on the **Faction tab** shows which province you stand in, every side's stake in every province, and whether you count as a resident (and if not, exactly why).

There is no button or item that claims a province. **Your faction claims one by living there**: settle enough residents around a fastened hall, lead the province on stake, and keep that lead for 72 hours.

### Becoming a resident

You count as one of your faction's residents in a province when all of these are true:

1. You have been on the faction's roster for **3 days**.
2. You have your **own bed**, fastened, held for **3 days**.
3. The bed is within **40 tiles of one of the faction's fastened halls**, or on a plot registered to the faction.
4. You have played **10 hours** in all on this land.
5. You played **4 active hours over at least 3 different days** in the last week. Time spent idle does not count.

A player counts once, however many characters they have there. If Province Holdings says you don't count, it names the step you are missing.

### Stake and leading

| Rule | Number |
| --- | --- |
| Each resident | 1 point |
| Your best hall in the province | +2 points per stage, never more than your residents there |
| To lead a province | at least 6 points from at least 4 people, 35% of all stake there, and 1.25 times the next side |
| Large groups | residents beyond the first 10 count for less |

Factions in one nation add their stake together and count as one side.

### Taking, holding and losing

1. **Take:** lead the province for **72 hours** in a row.
2. **Hold:** you keep it until a rival out-stakes you by the same margin for 72 hours. A ranked settlement raises that margin (see below).
3. **Slip:** if you drop below 6 points from 4 people, leaders are warned and the province is lost after **48 hours** unless you recover. Rebuild destroyed beds and halls quickly.
4. **Cap:** a side holds at most **3 provinces** of a land. Factions led by the same player count together for this cap.
5. **Timing:** a take or loss that falls due waits for the province's daily [defence window](#war-and-sieges), so you never lose land in the middle of the night.

Leaders are told whenever their hold slips, recovers, is challenged, taken or lost.

**What holding gives you:** working recruitment stations in that province, [sleep protection](#sleeping-safely) for members in their own beds, the right to build a ranked settlement, and the province's own resources (two or three provinces on each continent are [rich in an ore](Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md)).

**Plots fall to ruin:** a faction with fewer than **3** residents in a province for **14 days** in a row loses its registered plots there. The deeds go void and the buildings stay for anyone. Province Holdings warns you while the days run.

### Settlements: Camp to City

In a province your side holds, your best hall there is the **seat** of a settlement covering everything within 40 tiles. Click the hall to open its **Province ledger**, which ticks off what each rank needs.

| Rank | Hall | Residents | Rival needs | Time to recover | Weekly upkeep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Camp | council fire | 4 | 1.25x your stake | 48 h | free |
| Village | longhouse | 7 | 1.35x | 48 h | 50 silver |
| Town | town hall | 11 | 1.5x | 60 h | 150 silver |
| City | capitol | 16 | 1.6x | 72 h | 400 silver |

Higher ranks also need wells, roofed beds, guest beds, plots, a market stall, a wall ring with gates, a watchtower and more; the full checklist is in [Settlements: Camp to City](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#settlements-camp-to-city). Your side's best settlement adds 3 members per rank to the limit of every faction on your side, and a bed start inside a Town or City costs less than elsewhere. Upkeep comes from the strongbox; a second ranked province on the land costs 1.5 times and a third twice as much. Missing upkeep or a missing requirement drops the rank after 48 hours.

## Faction islands

Every faction can claim **one island across the sea** as its own. Unlike a province, nobody can take it by stake or siege.

1. Your **leader** sails a ship with a mast or engine and at least 2 crew from the coast to a land across the sea (the tiller's voyage menu; see [Boats](Guide_to_Boats.md#settle-claim-or-abandon)).
2. The leader plants a **colony charter** (8 planks) there. The land becomes your faction's claimed island.
3. Keep at least **3 members** with a fastened bed of their own there. If the island has fewer than 3 such members for **14 days** in a row, the claim lapses and any faction may claim it.

**Faction Island** on the **Faction tab** shows your island and who may land there. The leader and officers with the **papers** power set its border from any land:

| Border | Who may land |
| --- | --- |
| **Open to visitors** | Anyone |
| **Members and guests** (the default) | Members, named guests and visitor pass holders |
| **Members only** | Members only, whatever passes say |

- **Named guests** are added while standing beside the officer, and removed from the same menu.
- **Visitor passes** are granted at one of your recruitment stations: right-click it and choose **Grant Island Visitor Pass** for someone beside it, for 1 hour, 6 hours, 1 day, 3 days or 7 days. They only work while the border admits guests.
- Nobody else can sail there: the island is missing from their chart and ships refuse to set out with them aboard. Anyone ashore without leave is warned and put back on the mainland after **10 minutes**. Restrained captives stay put.

Changes reach every land within a minute or two. Recruitment stations on the island always work. Giving up the claim at the charter frees your faction to claim another island.

## Colonies in other lands

A faction lives in the land where it was founded, but its leader can found a **colony** in another land by carrying a **colony charter** (8 planks) there and planting it. The faction then exists in that land under the same name. Members arriving there join the colony, each colony adds room for 5 members, and members can start new characters at a settled colony. A colony on the mainland stands only while its charter does; a claimed island is the exception. See [Factions and colonies](Guide_to_Travel.md#factions-and-colonies).

## Sleeping safely

A member who goes to **Sleep** on a fastened bed, waits out its two minutes and then logs off cannot be harmed, stripped, moved or operated on until they come back, when the bed is:

- in a province your faction or nation **holds**, or
- on your faction's **claimed island**.

It does not apply on Gaia, to the factionless, in unheld provinces, or to anyone who logs off without using Sleep. A protected sleeper never blocks a doorway, and their bed can't be taken apart under them. If someone abuses it, adminhelp and staff can lift it until that player's next Sleep.

## Nations

In the wider world, factions can join into a **nation** with **Nation** on the **Faction tab**. Each faction keeps its own leader, research, members and treasury.

- **Found:** stand within a few steps of another faction's leader from your land and both agree. You become its head.
- **Apply:** the member factions vote; over half saying yes admits you. Applications lapse after 3 days.
- **Secede:** leave at any time (some governments ask for notice). You cannot reapply for 3 days.
- **Size:** a nation holds 3 factions, raised to 4, 6 or 8 by a **nation assembly** (30 planks) and some governments.
- **Money:** members can vote to adopt one member's coin as the common money.

**Why it matters for land:** a nation's factions pool their stake and share the 3-province cap, and treaties decide who may fight over provinces.

**Governments.** Every nation starts as a **confederation** and can switch under **Nation > Government** to a theocracy, monarchy, dictatorship, republic, democracy, merchant league, council of elders, feudal crown, constitutional monarchy or one-party state, each with its own head, elections, size bonus and rules for leaving. Citizens can overthrow any government through a **revolt** petition. See [Nation government](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#nation-government).

**Vassals.** A nation can become another's vassal by agreement, or have it imposed after losing 2 sieges in 14 days of war. A vassal's factions pay a weekly tribute of a tenth of their strongbox, up to 100 silver, to an overlord faction in the same land. Overlord and vassal count as allies, and after 14 days (28 under a feudal crown) a vassal can declare independence.

## Treaties and diplomacy

- **Between factions:** **Faction Treaties** lets anyone with the **diplomacy** power draft a treaty with up to five other factions. It comes into force when every party signs, and stays on record if renounced. The game does not enforce these treaties; the factions do.
- **Between nations:** **Nation > Foreign affairs** makes alliances, non-aggression pacts, trade treaties, war and peace. These are enforced: allies and pact partners can never take each other's provinces by stake, and a nation can only besiege another while they are at war.

## War and sieges

Taking a rival's province by force means a **siege** on its seat.

1. **Defence windows.** Every held province has a daily window of 3 hours (4 when the side holds two or more there), chosen by the leader with **Defence Window** among the server's busiest hours. A change takes 3 days. Provinces only change hands during their window.
2. **Declare.** A rival leader crafts a **war banner** (8 wood, **economy & production**), plants it in the province and clicks it. Their faction needs to be 7 days old, have 4 members with 2 hours played on the land, and hold **100 silver** in its strongbox as a deposit. Nations must be at war first. On Gaia only a Town or City can be besieged.
3. **Fight.** The siege starts in the first window at least 24 hours later. Attackers take the seat by standing within 3 tiles of it with no defender there for 10 minutes (Camp or unranked), 15 (Village), 20 (Town) or 30 (City). A defender at the seat pauses the count. Allies of either side join in.
4. **Outcome.** A taken seat suspends one rank's benefits for 7 days, and the province can't be besieged again for 3 days. Against a Camp or unranked seat, or a second win in 14 days, the holder's hold also starts slipping. If nobody turns up the deposit is lost; a failed or cancelled siege makes that side wait 3 days to try again.

The full rules are in [Sieges and defence windows](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#sieges-and-defence-windows). For fighting itself, see [Combat](Guide_to_Combat.md) and [Castles](Guide_to_Castles.md).

## Common questions

**How do I claim a province for my faction?**
There is no claim button. Build and fasten a faction hall in the province, get at least 4 members to become residents there (own bed within 40 tiles of the hall for 3 days, plus the play-time checks), and lead the province on stake for 72 hours. Check progress in **Province Holdings**.

**Why don't I count as a resident?**
Open **Province Holdings**: it names the missing step, such as a bed held for under 3 days, a bed too far from the hall, or not enough active hours this week.

**Why is our recruitment station idle?**
On a continent, a station only works in a province your side holds and with 3 of your own residents there. Examine it for the reason.

**How do I kick someone out?**
You can't by button. Members leave with **Abandon Faction**. Use your laws, courts and doors to deal with troublemakers.

**Our leader is gone. What now?**
If the seat is empty, any member can use **Become Faction Leader**. If the leader has been away more than 3 days, any member can call a leadership election in **Faction Offices**.

**Can enemies reach our island?**
Only if your border lets them. Set **Members only** in **Faction Island** to close it completely.

**Are we safe while logged off?**
Only if you used **Sleep** on your own fastened bed in a province your side holds or on your claimed island, and not on Gaia.
