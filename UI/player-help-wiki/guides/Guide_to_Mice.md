# Mice & Pests

Mice live in nests and eat from the food stores around them. This guide covers where they come from, how to keep your food safe, and how to get rid of them with cats, traps and poison.

```admonish info
Mice, nests, cats and traps work on every map with wild land. Flea-ridden nests and plague only happen in the Medieval and Imperial Ages.
```

## Where mice come from

Mice never appear out of rotten food. Rotten food only draws flies, and you will see "Flies buzz around it" when you examine it.

- **Wild burrows** sit on grass and dirt in temperate, dry, savanna, taiga and jungle land, out of sight. Each holds a few mice and does not grow in winter.
- **House nests** grow out of burrows. When a nest has plenty of food and room, it can start a new nest near a store it eats from. That is how mice move from the fields into your village.
- A house nest holds up to **8** mice. With no food in reach it loses a mouse at a time and disappears when it is empty.
- Mice come out at dusk and at night outdoors, and at any time indoors, a couple per nest. They run home when a person, cat or dog comes close.

Nests only start on dirt, grass, wooden floors or carpet, and never where someone can see. Stone, brick and tiled floors stay clear, and so does every floor above the ground floor: food stored upstairs is safe from mice.

## What mice eat

A nest eats from everything within **5 tiles** of it. Each meal also spoils one more food in the same store: it is renamed **gnawed**, gets tooth marks and gives food poisoning if eaten.

| Store | What mice do |
| --- | --- |
| Food or seeds on the floor, ripe crops, woven baskets, racks | Eat freely |
| Log chests, storage pits, wooden crates and closets | Gnaw in now and then |
| Clay storage jars, zeer pots, fridges, ice houses, freezers, metal, stone, glass and brick crates, anything you carry | Cannot get in |

A crop eaten by mice loses its harvest, but the plant lives and grows again. Examine a store to see how well it keeps mice out.

## Getting rid of mice

- **Dig out the nest.** Use a **shovel** or **digging stick** on it. It takes about 10 seconds and the mice scatter.
- **Starve it.** Move the food into clay jars or other mouse-proof stores. A house nest with nothing to eat dies out.
- **Cats.** **Wildcats** live in temperate, dry and savanna land and keep away from people. Hold raw meat or fish and they will come close; feed one by hand **6** times, at least 10 minutes apart, and it becomes a tame cat. Two tame cats together raise kittens now and then. Every cat within 7 tiles of a nest kills a mouse each time the nest grows and stops the nest spreading. A dog counts for half a cat.
- **Snap mousetrap.** Craft it from iron once **Iron Tools** is researched. Use it in your hand to set it, then put it on the floor near the nest. It catches one mouse; use it in hand to drop the dead mouse and set it again. It stings a barefoot person who steps on it.
- **Baited deadfall.** A [deadfall](Guide_to_Trapping.md) can also be set on any indoor floor. Bait the set deadfall with a bit of food and it falls on a mouse from a nest nearby. Indoors it catches nothing else.
- **Poison bait.** Use grain (wheat, oat, barley or rice) with a **green potato**, **poisonous frog meat**, or a container holding at least 5 units of **arsenic**, **cyanide** or **solanine**. Put the bait on the floor within 5 tiles of the nest. Each bait kills a few mice at a time, three times over. It is real poison, so keep it away from people and pets.

## Fleas and plague

In the Medieval and Imperial Ages, a house nest kept full of mice for a long time becomes **flea-ridden**; examining the nest says so.

- Anyone sleeping or lying within 3 tiles of a flea-ridden nest can catch **plague**.
- Digging one out bare-handed can give you plague too. Wear gloves.
- Thinning the nest below 5 mice gets rid of the fleas. Wild burrows never get fleas.
- A Strong Immune System makes plague from fleas much less likely; a Weak one makes it more likely.

Walking past or touching a mouse never gives you plague. See [Medical](Guide_to_Medical.md#plague) for treatment.

## Tips

- Keep grain and seed in clay storage jars and lay stone or brick floors in your stores.
- Do not sleep in the same room as a big nest.
- One tame cat in a granary does more than a dozen traps.
