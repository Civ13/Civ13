# Guide to Accolades and Characters

***"A life well lived leaves more than one story."***

Accolades are lifetime achievements that belong to your BYOND account, not to one character. Each one is earned and claimed once, ever, and gives you **lifetime points**. You spend those points on extra characters, so you can play someone new without giving up the character you already have.

Accolades are earned on persistent worlds such as Gaia. Guests and battle, event and tutorial worlds earn none, except **First Steps**, which you get by finishing the tutorial.

## The Accolades window

Open the **OOC** tab and click **Accolades**. The window shows:

- your balance of lifetime points;
- accolades you have earned but not claimed yet, each with a **Claim** button;
- every accolade, how it is earned, its points and whether you have claimed it.

An accolade you have earned waits for you until you claim it, in any land. Once claimed, its points are yours for good.

| World Traveller | Live in every land of the world | 40 |

Play time only counts while you are connected and your character is awake. Hunter never counts tamed animals or kept livestock. Founder, Teacher and Magistrate only count other players who have played a few hours of their own, and never your own other characters.

## Extra characters

Open the **OOC** tab and click **Characters**. Every account has one character. You can unlock up to three more:

| Character | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| 2nd | 100 lifetime points |
| 3rd | 200 lifetime points |
| 4th | 300 lifetime points |

Slots are unlocked in order, and spent points stay spent. Claimed accolades are never taken away by unlocking.

**Only Discord-verified accounts can unlock or play extra characters.** Link yours with `/approveme` in the Civ13 Discord, or with **Verify Discord** in the OOC tab. If an account stops being verified, it plays its first character, and its other characters stay asleep until it is verified again.

### Switching characters

You play one character at a time. Press **Play** on another character in the Characters window to switch to them:

- Your current character must be asleep in their own bed (use **Sleep** in your bed first). From the lobby, you can also switch once your character has been left alone for 30 minutes.
- A character you leave cannot be played again for an hour.
- You cannot switch while your character is restrained, jailed, wanted or was hurt in the last ten minutes.

The character you leave stays where they rest, with their own bed, home and belongings. Each character has their own home land.

## Kin

Your characters are **kin**, and that is public: anyone who examines one of them sees the names of the others. A court's warrant or outlawry against one of your characters shows on your other characters too.

Your characters share one leadership seat. While one of them leads a faction, none of the others can found or lead another one.

Your characters count as one person for a province's stake and for the members a faction needs to declare a siege, however many of them live there.

Factions led by the same player share one limit on the provinces they can hold in a land, and no group of factions can hold more than half of a land's provinces.

Splitting one group into several factions, or using kin, to get around claim limits is structuring. It is not allowed, and admins will step in to break it up.
