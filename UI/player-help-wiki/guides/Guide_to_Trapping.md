# Trapping

Traps catch food while you are off doing something else. Set a line of them on your way out and check them on the way back. You can build all of them from the first age on.

## Snares and deadfalls

Hold the snare or deadfall and click the ground next to you to set it. After that it works on its own:

* About every 10 minutes it has a chance to catch something. The more wild animals live nearby, the better the chance. In winter it is halved.
* A **snare** catches rabbits and sometimes a fox. A **deadfall** catches rabbits, foxes and now and then a wild boar.
* Examine a trap to see whether it has caught anything. Click it with an empty hand to take the catch out, and butcher it as usual.
* **Check your traps every day.** A catch left more than a day is eaten by scavengers.
* Click a sprung trap with an empty hand to set it again. Click a set trap with an empty hand to pick it back up.

Set traps away from your camp, where animals actually live.

## Fish weirs

<img src="../images/Fish_weir.png"> A **fish weir** is a fence of stakes and wicker that fish swim into and can't find their way out of. Set the weir stakes in shallow fresh water, such as a river or lake. Deep water, salt water and ice don't work.

* About every 15 minutes there is a good chance another fish is caught, fewer in winter.
* It holds up to 4 fish, salmon or ordinary fish. Click it with an empty hand to scoop them out.
* The fish come from the stretch of river the weir stands in. If that water has been fished out, the weir stays empty until the fish return; look at the weir or the water to tell.
* Click an empty weir with an empty hand to pull it up and move it.

Unlike a [fish pen](Guide_to_Fishing.md#fish-pens), a weir needs no stocking or feeding, but it never holds many fish.

## Pit traps

<img src="../images/Staked_pit.png"> A **pit trap** is a hole hidden under branches. It catches people as well as animals.

1. Hold a **shovel** or **digging stick** in one hand and the **pit cover** in the other.
2. Click the open ground next to you. Digging takes a while; then the pit is hidden under the branches.
3. For a deadlier pit, uncover it and use **punji sticks** on it to plant stakes at the bottom, then cover it again with a pit cover.

To anyone else a covered pit looks like a few branches on the ground. **You and your tribe know where it is and never fall in.** Anyone else who walks onto it, and any animal the size of a deer or bigger, falls in:

* A person is knocked down and hurt, and badly hurt if there are stakes. They are stuck until they climb out (resist) or someone pulls them out (click them with an empty hand).
* An animal is stuck for a good while, which gives hunters time to reach it.
* Walk carefully (walk intent) through unfamiliar ground: you might feel the branches give in time and catch yourself.

A pit stays open after it catches something. Lay a new pit cover over it to set it again, or fill it in with a shovel or digging stick.
