## Settings and controls

### How do I turn off character animations?

Walking, running and sprinting are smooth, with no hop on each step. The motions that remain, such as the tilt when you are hit or fall, the heave when you haul something heavy and the crouch when you sneak, are set by the server for everyone. There is no personal setting for this yet. Movement itself can be changed: see the next question.

### How do I switch to tile-by-tile movement?

Holding a movement key always slides you smoothly. **Tap To Step** decides what a quick tap does: when it is on (the default), a tap moves you exactly one tile and you always finish the tile you were stepping onto. When it is off, you stop exactly where you let go of the key. Click the footprint button on your HUD, or open **Settings > Controls and HUD** (also in the Game Menu on **Esc**). See [Hotkeys](Hotkeys.md).

### How do I hide or move the big name that follows my mouse?

Use **Toggle Hover Text** in the **OOC** tab to hide it, **Hover Text Position** to show it in a line at the bottom of the screen instead, and **Hover Text Size** to shrink it. The same choices are in **Settings > Controls and HUD**. See [Hover names](guides/Guide_to_HUD.md#hover-names).

### The HUD is too big. How do I make it smaller?

Use **HUD Scale** to make the whole HUD bigger or smaller. It is separate from the hover text size. See [HUD Scale](guides/Guide_to_HUD.md#hud-scale).

### How do I change a key binding?

Open **Settings > Controls and HUD** and choose the key bindings. The default keys are listed in [Hotkeys](Hotkeys.md).

### How do I get out of the practice room?

Press **Esc** and choose **Leave practice room**, or use **Leave Practice Room** in the Practice group at the top of the IC tab. See [How to Play](New_Player_Guide.md).

## Help and bug reports

### Where do I see the answers from Ask for help?

**Ask for help** (in the **Help!** tab, or in the Game Menu on **Esc**) opens its own window. Your question, the answer and any follow-up questions all stay in that window. Nothing is answered in chat, so keep the window open until the answer arrives.

### How do I get help from a person?

Use mentorhelp for gameplay questions, and adminhelp (**F1**) for rule concerns or problems such as being stuck or losing your character. See [How to Play](New_Player_Guide.md) and [Rules](rules/Rules.md).

### How do I report a bug?

Use **Help > Report bug** in the menu bar, the **Report Bug** verb in the **Help!** tab, or the Game Menu on **Esc**. Give a one-line summary, then describe what happened and how to make it happen again. Reporting it in OOC alone is easy to miss.

## Your first tools

### How do I make my first axe?

1. Click a large rock with an empty hand on **Grab intent** to gather flint.
2. Knap it: hold a stone or a second flint, use it on the flint and choose **Sharp flake**.
3. Click a tree with an empty hand on **Harm intent** to pull off a branch.
4. Click the branch with an empty hand on **Grab intent** to clear the leaves, then on **Harm intent** to pull out the twigs, and sharpen the stick with the flint.
5. Get a binding (see the next question), then use the sharpened flint on the sharpened stick.

See the [Starter Guide](Starter_Guide.md) and [Flint](guides/Guide_to_Flint.md).

### How do I get twine or a binding?

Use the sharpened flint on grass or tall grass for **grass cuttings**, then hold two and use them together to twist **crude twine**. Butchering an animal gives **sinew**, which lasts longer than twine. See the [Starter Guide](Starter_Guide.md).

### How do I make a torch?

Craft a **handle** from wood, then click it with a piece of animal fat, or with a container holding at least 5 units of olive oil, fat oil, corn oil or petroleum. Click the torch in hand to light it. One piece of fat burns for about 10 minutes. See [Lighting](Starter_Guide.md).

### Where do I get a manual, such as the Basic Toolmaking manual? Why can't I craft a roof or a bed?

Crafting checks what **you** know, so most recipes need you to learn their subject first, such as **Basic Toolmaking**.

- **Where research is written into books:** the founder of a faction receives the **Stone Age master book**. Hold it, use it and press **Study** beside a chapter. Pencils, paper and blank books need no knowledge. See [Books and Scholars](guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md#getting-started).
- **Where research gives recipe manuals:** when your faction finishes researching a subject, a manual for it, such as the **Basic Toolmaking manual**, appears at your faction's research bench. Use it in hand to study its recipes; it can be used again by others. Someone who knows the recipes can copy a manual with **Write recipe manual** in the IC tab, holding a pen and a blank sheet of paper or parchment for two minutes.

## Research and scholarship

### How do I start research?

Build a **research bench** (8 soft wood), put a book on it, click a subject and press **Research this here**. Stay within three tiles of the bench with a pencil, pen or writing slate while it works. A finished chapter is written into the book and still has to be studied by whoever wants it. See [Research benches](guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md#research-benches).

### How do I raise my Engineering, Agriculture or other scholarship?

You gain scholarship by working at a research bench, studying chapters, writing them, and by hands-on work that goes into a book. Mastering a body skill also teaches its discipline: crafting teaches Engineering, farming and hunting teach Agriculture, medical teaches Medicine, philosophy teaches Basic Mathematics, and weapon skills teach Military Science. The **Scholarship** tab shows your levels. See [Scholarship](guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md#scholarship) and [Learning by doing](guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md#learning-by-doing).

### Why is my research so slow?

Work beside the bench runs at full pace; two or three tiles away it runs at three quarters. Skill above the chapter's requirement makes research up to twice as fast. Using **samples** of what the subject unlocks on the bench speeds it up, and a field of study speeds up its own discipline. See [Research benches](guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md#research-benches) and [Fields of study](guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md#fields-of-study).

## Staying alive

### Is the water safe to drink?

Raw water from puddles, rivers and lakes should be boiled first. Wells have disease-free fresh water, unless there is excrement within four tiles of the well. Never drink or boil saltwater. See [Thirst](Starter_Guide.md#thirst).

### I can't drink or eat. What am I doing wrong?

- **Drinking:** hold the cup, mug, bottle or bucket with its lid off, switch to **Help intent**, aim at your **mouth** and click yourself. See [Thirst](Starter_Guide.md#thirst).
- **Eating:** hold the food and click yourself. A mask or helmet over your mouth gets in the way, and the message names it. You also cannot eat when you are already full, and a vegan or carnivore character refuses the wrong kind of food. See [Cooking](guides/Guide_to_Cooking.md).

### How do I dry off?

Get out of the rain and stand within two tiles of a lit fire, which dries you about three times faster. The **Wring Out Clothes** verb in the IC tab leaves soaked clothes only damp, but not while you are still standing in water. See [Rain and wet clothes](guides/Guide_to_Survival.md#rain-and-wet-clothes).

### How do I wash myself?

Undress, stand in fresh water and use **Bathe** in the IC tab. Running river water gets you properly clean, and soap makes it a full wash. A bucket of clean water works too, but only part of the way. See [Hygiene](guides/Guide_to_Hygiene.md).

### I'm sick. What do I do?

Rest in a bed to recover faster and keep away from people who are coughing. See [Medical](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md) for each illness and its treatment.

## Getting around

### How do I climb up, climb down or get out of a hole?

- **Up:** face a wall with a free hand and use **IC > Climb Up**, or click the wall with grab intent.
- **Down:** stand at the edge of an upper floor with a free hand and click the open air beside you with grab intent, or use **IC > Climb Down**. Walking off the edge is a hard fall.
- **Ladder:** craft a wooden ladder (4 wood) and use it on the open air next to an upper floor, or on the ground under open space. Click either end to climb.

See [Getting between storeys](guides/Guide_to_Construction.md#getting-between-storeys).

### I can't move at all. Am I stuck?

Most often the world is saving. While your level is being saved you are held still for a moment, but you can still look around and chat, and hunger, thirst and bleeding pause too. A message tells you when your level is saved. If you still cannot move after that, try reconnecting, then adminhelp. See [When the world saves](Starter_Guide.md#when-the-world-saves).

### How do I stop pulling something, or get off a horse?

Press **Delete** to stop pulling. To dismount, click the horse with an empty hand. See [Hotkeys](Hotkeys.md) and [Animals](guides/Guide_to_Animals.md).

### How do I find other players?

Hold leather or cloth and click a lit campfire under the open sky to send a **smoke signal**. Everyone outdoors a long way off is told which way to walk to reach you. See [Smoke signals](guides/Guide_to_Survival.md#smoke-signals).

## Factions, people and languages

### How do I invite someone to my faction?

Get close to them, right-click them and choose **Recruit**. The leader and members with the **Recruitment** permission can recruit. A faction can also build a **recruitment station** from 10 planks and take members through it. See [Civilizations & Nomads](gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#recruitment-stations).

### How do I change which language I speak, or learn a new one?

Use **Set Default Language** in the IC tab to choose which language you speak, and **Check Known Languages** to see what you know. You learn a language by listening to it, from lessons (a fluent speaker uses **Teach Language** next to you), and from language books. See [Peoples and Languages](guides/Guide_to_Peoples_and_Languages.md#learning-a-language).

## Travel between lands

### How do I travel to another land?

Walk across a border where the land carries on, sail, fly or take a trail. Your character lives in one land at a time, and travelling moves your home with you. See [Travel Between Lands](guides/Guide_to_Travel.md) and [Boats](guides/Guide_to_Boats.md).

### Why did my new character start in a different land from the one I joined?

New characters start on the mainland, [Gaia](maps/Gaia.md). If you join a land that is not your character's home, the game tells you which land your character belongs to and links you to it. See [Your home land](guides/Guide_to_Travel.md#your-home-land).

## Restarts, saves and lost characters

### I died or lost my character, traits or languages during a restart. What now?

Adminhelp (**F1**) and say what happened and when. Staff can look into it. If your character died normally, see [If you die](guides/Guide_to_Your_First_Day.md#if-you-die) for what a new character can take back.

### How do I check my traits?

Your traits are listed under **Your traits** in character setup. See [Character & Skills](guides/Guide_to_Character.md).
